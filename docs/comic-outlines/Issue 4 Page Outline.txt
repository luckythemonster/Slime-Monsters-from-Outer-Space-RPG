Issue #4: Battle of the Bands
Part 1 Page-by-Page Outline
PAGE 1 – KEEP AUSTIN WEIRD (Beat 1)
  ●​ Toon Level: 2 (Sci-Fi Worldbuilding)
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1 (Wide Splash): Establish the setting: South by South-Wasteland. It is a
     sprawling, neon-soaked, dusty desert metropolis that looks like a music festival crossbred
     with a dystopian bazaar. The sky is choked with advertising blimps.
  ●​ Panel 2: The streets are absolute chaos. A food truck made out of a rusted Vanguard
     transport tank is serving "Quantum Brisket." Feral, autonomous electric scooters weave
     aggressively through the crowds like pack hunters.
  ●​ Panel 3: Down in the crowd, the band is struggling. The Texas-asteroid sun is beating
     down on them. Lucky and Diego are dragging Diego's massive, rusted amp up a dusty
     hill. They look absolutely exhausted and soaked in sweat.
  ●​ Panel 4: Phoenix is trailing behind, clutching their phone. The screen blinks a massive,
     flashing warning: EVICTION IN 24 HOURS. Phoenix's gray slime is practically
     evaporating in the heat.
PAGE 2 – THE INDUSTRY SCOUT (Beat 1 Cont.)
  ●​ Toon Level: 2
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Suddenly, a man in a blindingly bright, reflective silver suit steps directly into
     their path, blocking the amp. He is wearing high-end AR glasses and holding a glowing
     clipboard. He looks overly manicured and entirely artificial.
  ●​ Panel 2: The Scout points aggressively at Lucky with a glowing stylus.
        ○​ Scout: "Whoa! Stop the metrics! Look at this organic grit! You guys are fully
             un-optimized! I love it!"
  ●​ Panel 3: Diego wipes his forehead, looking confused.
        ○​ Diego: "Uh... thanks, man. We're just trying to find the loading dock for the battle of
             the bands."
  ●​ Panel 4: The Scout throws his arm around Diego's shoulder, leaning in uncomfortably
     close.
        ○​ Scout: "I can streamline that journey, brother. I'm scouting raw IP for the Main
             Stage. We need authentic, un-synergized noise to pivot our demographic."
PAGE 3 – THE PITCH (Beat 2)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Lucky shoves the Scout's arm off of Diego. Lucky is glowing a hot, irritated
     magenta.
        ○​ Lucky: "We don't care about your demographic pivot. We just need to know if the
             prize money is real."
  ●​ Panel 2: The Scout holds up his glowing clipboard, tapping it. A hologram of a golden,
     shimmering Vanguard Corporate Coupon projects into the air.
        ○​ Scout: "Oh, the ROI is massive! The grand prize is a fully validated,
            non-transferable corporate coupon. Good for exactly one standard month's rent at
            any Vanguard-approved housing sector."
  ●​ Panel 3: Phoenix's eyes go wide behind their thick glasses. The gray slime immediately
     perks up, reaching desperately toward the holographic coupon.
        ○​ Phoenix: "My brain thinks that is the most beautiful thing I have ever seen."
  ●​ Panel 4: The Scout smiles, a terrifyingly white, perfectly straight grin.
        ○​ Scout: "Just sign right here to enter the bracket. Fast-track your synergy."
PAGE 4 – THE CATCH (Beat 3)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Lucky grabs the stylus, ready to aggressively scribble their signature.
        ○​ Lucky: "Whatever. If it pays Phoenix's rent, we'll play."
  ●​ Panel 2: Before the stylus touches the board, Diego squints at the extremely tiny, scrolling
     fine print at the bottom of the holographic contract.
        ○​ Diego: "Hold up, man. What's this clause about an 'Acoustic Embargo'?"
  ●​ Panel 3: The Scout waves his hand dismissively, his smile never wavering.
        ○​ Scout: "Standard industry protection! By accepting the prize, you legally agree to a
            thirty-day non-compete gag order. You can't play, broadcast, or synthesize music for
            a month, or the coupon is voided and the Vanguard repossesses your organs!"
  ●​ Panel 4: Close up on Lucky. The pink slime freezes in absolute horror. The stylus slips
     from their hand.
PAGE 5 – THE SACRIFICE (Beat 3 Cont.)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Lucky looks at the Scout, appalled.
        ○​ Lucky: "A month?! Without playing? I'll literally melt! That's a death sentence for my
            soul!"
  ●​ Panel 2: Phoenix steps forward. They look at Lucky. Phoenix is a puddle of exhausted,
     deeply sad gray slime, dripping onto the dusty Austin street.
        ○​ Phoenix: "Lucky... my landlord is going to change the locks tomorrow morning."
  ●​ Panel 3: Lucky looks at Phoenix. The empathy hits hard. The hot magenta fades into a
     resolute, dull pink. Lucky reaches down and picks the stylus back up.
  ●​ Panel 4: Lucky aggressively signs the clipboard.
        ○​ Lucky: "Fine. We'll do it. Let's go sell out."
  ●​ Caption Box (Bottom Corner): "Next: Princess Bajonka vs. Pete."

  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1 (Wide): Interior of Phoenix’s apartment (the "base"). It’s cramped and messy.

     cleaning a heavy blaster pistol. A portable speaker plays a soothing, acoustic Joni
     Mitchell track.
  ●​ Panel 2: Standing in the middle of the room, barking loudly at a completely blank patch of
     drywall, is PETE. He is a large, unkempt dog that literally just looks like the head of a dirty
     industrial mop with a pink tongue hanging out.
  ●​ Panel 3: Shannon smiles warmly, tossing a treat at Pete. It bounces off his head because
     he isn't paying attention.
        ○​ Shannon: "Good boy, Pete. You keep an eye on that wall."
  ●​ Panel 4: Pan up to the top of the kitchen cabinets. Hidden in the deep shadows, Bajonka
     is watching them. Her eyes narrow into severe, glowing slits. The disrespect is palpable.
PAGE 7 – THE COUNTER-OFFENSIVE (Beat 2)
  ●​ Toon Level: 2 (Visual Comedy)
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Bajonka slips silently down the back of the cabinets into the kitchen. She nudges
     a loose floorboard aside with her nose, revealing a hidden, foam-lined stash box of heavy
     Vanguard ordnance.
  ●​ Panel 2: Extreme close-up. Bajonka’s paws delicately extract a dull green, curved
     anti-personnel mine (a sci-fi Claymore). The stenciled yellow lettering reads: FRONT
     TOWARD ENEMY.
  ●​ Panel 3: Bajonka is sitting in the center of the kitchen floor, wearing a tiny tactical
     headband. She holds a pair of wire strippers in her mouth, expertly splicing a detonator
     wire to a bright neon-pink, deeply chewed rubber squeaky toy.
  ●​ Panel 4: The trap is set. The squeaky toy sits in the middle of the linoleum. A faint, red
     trip-laser spans across the kitchen threshold. Bajonka retreats under the kitchen table to
     observe.
PAGE 8 – THE FOG OF WAR (Beat 3)
  ●​ Toon Level: 2
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Pete lumbers toward the kitchen, his mop-hair bouncing. Bajonka watches from
     under the table, her body tense, waiting for the perfect strike.
  ●​ Panel 2: Pete steps into the kitchen. Incredibly, his massive, clumsy paws step exactly
     over the red trip-laser without him even noticing.
  ●​ Panel 3: Pete stops right next to the wired squeaky toy. Bajonka’s eyes widen in
     anticipation.
  ●​ Panel 4: Pete stares at the squeaky toy for three seconds. Then, distracted by a floating
     dust mote, he turns completely around and lumbers over to sniff the refrigerator door

PAGE 9 – FRIENDLY FIRE (Beat 3 Cont.)
  ●​ Toon Level: 3 (Slapstick)
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Shannon walks into the kitchen threshold, stretching her arms and yawning.
        ○​ Shannon: "Hey Pete, you hungry? Let me grab—"
  ●​ Panel 2: Shannon kicks off her heavy combat boots. One of the boots flies through the
     air, landing directly on the neon-pink squeaky toy. SFX: SQUEAK.
  ●​ Panel 3 (Large): KABOOM! A massive, concussive blast of smoke, sparks, and shredded

  ●​ Panel 4: The smoke clears slightly. The kitchen is scorched black. Cabinet doors are
     hanging by single hinges. Shannon is coughing, her face covered in soot. Pete is sitting

     Bajonka slowly drags a paw down her face in utter defeat.
  ●​ Caption Box (Bottom Corner): "Next: Backstage with the Boss."
