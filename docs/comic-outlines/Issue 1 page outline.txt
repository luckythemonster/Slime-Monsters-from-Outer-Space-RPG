Issue #1: Gig in the Potato Belt
Part 1 Page-by-Page Outline
PAGE 1 – THE TOUR VEHICLE (Beat 1)
  ●​ Toon Level: 2 (Rule of Funny)
  ●​ 1 Panel (Full Page Splash).
  ●​ The Scene: Deep, beautiful, cosmic space. Swirling purple nebulas and distant, glowing
     star systems.
  ●​ The Action: Rocketing through the center of the majestic cosmos is a rusted, beat-up,
     late-90s Earth passenger van. It has retro-fitted thrusters crudely welded to the rear
     bumper, duct tape on the side mirror, and Diego's dripping, neon-green triangle slime
     spiral spray-painted on the side door. It is the least aerodynamic spacecraft in the
     universe.
  ●​ Title Card: SLIME MONSTERS FROM OUTER SPACE - ISSUE #1: GIG IN THE
     POTATO BELT.
PAGE 2 – THE PILOT (Beat 1 Cont.)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Interior of the space-van. It is crammed with amps, drum hardware, and empty
     coffee cups. Phoenix is in the driver's seat, both hands gripping the steering wheel,
     looking absolutely miserable. Piloting an analog van through the vacuum of space is
     physically exhausting.
  ●​ Panel 2: Lucky is in the passenger seat, glowing a stressed pink, looking at a Vanguard
     navigation map on a cracked tablet.
        ○​ Lucky: "Take the next orbital exit, Phoenix. And don't clip the toll booth, I don't want
            to know who Bajonka stole this van from."
  ●​ Panel 3: In the back seat, Bajonka is curled up on a snare drum case, peacefully
     sleeping. Diego is casually looking out the window, completely unbothered by the fact that
     they are in space.
        ○​ Diego: "Man, the stars out here are heavy. Really makes you feel small, you know?"
  ●​ Panel 4: Close up on Phoenix's face. They are sweating slightly, their gray slime bubbling
     with stress at the edges of their thick glasses.
        ○​ Phoenix (deadpan): "My brain thinks if I have to manually parallel park this thing in
            zero gravity, I am going to walk into the sun."
PAGE 3 – THE POTATO BELT (Beat 2)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1 (Wide): The space-van breaks through a thick, smoggy, yellowish atmosphere.
     Below them is the Potato Belt. It is a terrifying, industrial agricultural nightmare. Endless,
     perfectly straight rows of glowing, radioactive-looking cosmic crops stretch to the horizon.
  ●​ Panel 2: The sky is completely blotted out by massive, hovering Vanguard
     combine-harvesters. They look like floating military fortresses bristling with exhaust pipes,
     threshers, and searchlights.
  ●​ Panel 3: Inside the van, Warner whistles, leaning up between the front seats.
        ○​ Warner: "Wow. They really gentrified the farming sector. Real dystopian vibe."
  ●​ Panel 4: Lucky points out the windshield toward a dark, muddy trench running between
     two massive fields of glowing crops.
        ○​ Lucky: "There. The promoter said the underground barn is beneath Sector 4. Bring
             us down."
PAGE 4 – THE LANDING (Beat 3)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: The van descends into the trench. SQUELCH. It lands heavily in deep, thick
     agricultural mud. The rusted suspension groans loudly.
  ●​ Panel 2: The thrusters hiss and power down. Phoenix immediately slumps forward,
     resting their gray, gelatinous forehead against the center of the steering wheel.
        ○​ Phoenix: "We're here. Please don't make me drive back."
  ●​ Panel 3: Diego pops the sliding side door open. A thick wave of green, intensely humid
     agricultural fog rolls into the van.
  ●​ Panel 4: The band steps out into the mud. Warner slings his stick bag over his shoulder.
     Lucky pulls their custom laser-guitar case out of the back. Diego grabs his red
     Stratocaster.
PAGE 5 – THE HUMIDITY (Beat 3 Cont.)
  ●​ Toon Level: 2 (Slime Physics)
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Extreme close-up of Phoenix stepping out of the driver's side door. The intense,
     greenhouse-level humidity of the Potato Belt hits them like a physical wall.
  ●​ Panel 2: Phoenix immediately begins melting. The gray slime rapidly loses its sharp
     edges, dripping down their forehead, bubbling furiously at their wrists. Their bulky hoodie
     starts to sag under the weight of the moisture.
  ●​ Panel 3: Phoenix leans heavily against the side of the muddy van, looking like a melting
     gray candle. They push up their glasses, which are completely fogged over.
        ○​ Phoenix: "My brain thinks I am going to become a puddle. Put me in a Tupperware
             container."
  ●​ Panel 4: Lucky looks ahead through the thick fog. A faint, glowing red light marks the
     entrance to a rusted, half-buried underground silo. The thumping vibration of bass can be
     felt through the mud.
        ○​ Lucky: "Hold it together, Phoenix. We load in, we play fast, we get paid."
  ●​ Caption Box (Bottom Corner): "Next: Load-In & The Khakis."


PAGE 6 – THE UNDERGROUND BARN (Beat 1)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: The band hauls their gear down a steep, muddy, dimly lit ramp leading beneath the
crop fields. Phoenix is practically dragging their bass amp, leaving a trail of gray slime and mud
behind them. Bajonka trots past them with a massive snare drum balanced effortlessly on her
head.
• Panel 2 (Wide): Interior of the hollowed-out underground grain silo. It is packed, loud, and
smells like cheap beer, ozone, and wet dirt. The lighting is harsh and industrial.

• Panel 3: Focus on the crowd. They are rough. Overworked, grimy agricultural laborers with
heavy, crude cybernetic farming limbs (e.g., a tractor-tiller instead of a left hand). They look
exhausted, mean, and desperate to blow off some steam.

• Panel 4: Diego walks through the crowd, carrying his red Stratocaster case. He smiles and
throws a peace sign at a massive, terrifying worker with a welding-torch arm.

• Diego: "Right on. Good vibes tonight, brothers."

• Worker (grunting, terrifying): "I am going to bite someone."

PAGE 7 – THE NARCS (Beat 2)

• Toon Level: 2 (Visual Comedy)

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Lucky is carrying their laser-guitar case, their pink slime glowing faintly in the dim
lighting. They scan the room with hyper-vigilant punk paranoia. Suddenly, they freeze.

• Lucky: "Oh, you have got to be kidding me."

• Panel 2: Point-of-view from Lucky. Standing awkwardly near the edge of the pit area are two
undercover Red Star Vanguard agents. They are failing miserably at blending in. They are
wearing stiff, pressed khakis, tucked-in polo shirts, Vanguard-issue combat boots, and holding
clipboards.

• Panel 3: Close-up on the agents. One of them is adjusting a hidden earpiece, looking
completely terrified of the surrounding punks. The other is aggressively standing completely still,
deliberately not headbanging to the opening band's noise.

• Panel 4: Lucky points them out to the rest of the band.

• Lucky: "Feds. 3 o'clock. They look like they're about to audit a mosh pit."

PAGE 8 – VISA ISSUES (Beat 3)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Diego follows Lucky's gaze. His chill, slacker demeanor instantly evaporates. His
eyes go wide behind his thick-rimmed glasses.

• Diego: "Wait... Vanguard? Like, the actual galactic state authorities?"
• Panel 2: Diego clutches his Stratocaster case to his chest, looking around nervously.

• Diego: "Man, I do not have an interstellar visa. I'm literally an Earth immigrant. If they scan me,
I'm getting deported to a labor camp on a moon I can't pronounce."

• Panel 3: Diego takes a deep breath, visibly trying to force himself to calm down. He closes his
eyes and holds up two fingers.

• Diego: "Okay. Okay. Just send them positive vibrations, man. Match their frequency with
peace."

• Panel 4: Lucky slowly turns to look at Diego. Lucky's slime instantly boils with rage, turning a
blinding, radioactive magenta. The spikes return.

• Lucky: "Diego. If you send a cop a 'positive vibration,' I will personally launch you into the sun."

PAGE 9 – TAKING THE STAGE (Beat 3 Cont.)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: The band sets up on the crude wooden stage made of repurposed shipping pallets.
Bajonka hops up onto a speaker monitor, her ears perked up, already analyzing the room's
emergency exits.

• Panel 2: Phoenix plugs in their short-scale bass. The humidity is still crushing them; they look
miserable, wiping a thick layer of gray slime-sweat from their glasses.

• Phoenix: "My brain thinks we should play twice as fast so we can get paid and leave before the
feds finish their paperwork."

• Panel 3: Warner sits behind the drum kit, adjusting his cymbals, completely unbothered by the
Vanguard presence. He casually spins a drumstick, looking completely at home in the dingy
club.

• Panel 4: Lucky steps up to the microphone. Their pink slime is bubbling with adrenaline and
defiance. The crowd roars—a chaotic, threatening mix of cheers and hostile boos.

• Caption Box (Bottom Corner): "Next: The Trigger."

PAGE 10 – "MONSTERS" (Beat 1)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Lucky grabs the rusted microphone stand, pulling it close. They glare out at the

• Lucky (screaming into the mic): "We are the Slime Monsters From Outer Space! This one's

PAGE 11 – THE PIT (Beat 2)

• Toon Level: 2 (Sound/Physics Visualization)

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: A massive, visible shockwave of thick, fuzzy sound erupts from the amps. It physically
ripples through the humid air of the barn.

• Panel 2: The sheer acoustic force slams into the crowd. The exhausted, angry laborers
instantly lose their minds. The crowd erupts into a violent, joyful, incredibly dangerous mosh pit.
Cybernetic tractor-arms and heavy boots are flying everywhere.

• Panel 3: The heavy sound waves hit the wooden support beams of the underground silo. The
wood groans and splinters. Dust falls from the ceiling.

• Panel 4: The two undercover Vanguard agents in khakis are immediately caught in the
crossfire. They are violently jostled and pinballed around the edge of the pit, looking absolutely
terrified as one of their clipboards gets trampled into the mud.

PAGE 12 – SLIME POWER (Beat 3)

• Toon Level: 2 (Slime Physics)

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Back on stage, the band is locking in. Warner is hammering the drums, sweat flying.
Phoenix is playing a heavy, driving bassline, holding the rhythm together despite melting.

• Panel 2: Lucky is shredding on the custom laser-guitar. They close their eyes, completely lost
in the music and the raw adrenaline of the performance. Their neon pink slime flares up
brilliantly.

• Panel 3 (Large): Lucky needs to tweak an effects knob on their pedalboard, but they are too
busy nailing a riff high up on the guitar neck. Without even thinking, Lucky uses their slime
powers. Their right leg physically liquefies, stretches, and elongates like a glowing pink rubber
band, snapping across the stage to tweak the knob with a slimy toe before snapping right back
into shape.

• Panel 4: It’s a spectacular, undeniably alien display of physics-breaking biology.

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 3: The agent frantically grabs his lapel, pressing the hidden comms button on his collar.

• Agent: "Command! Positive ID! It's the missing Vanguard biological asset! The pink slime!"

• Panel 4: The agent ducks as a flying farmhand sails past his head in the pit.

PAGE 14 – THE BREACH (Beat 1)

• Toon Level: 3 (High Action)

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: The song is ripping. The crowd is a sweaty, violent blur. Lucky is wailing on the
laser-guitar.

• Panel 2 (Large): BOOM! The massive, rusted metal roof of the underground barn violently
detonates inward. Giant chunks of debris rain down on the mosh pit.

• Panel 3: Harsh, blinding red Vanguard emergency searchlights cut through the dust and
humidity, shining directly down onto the crude wooden stage.

• Panel 4: Three Vanguard Riot Drones—hovering, heavy armored spheres bristling with

PAGE 15 – THE BAND REACTS (Beat 2)

• Toon Level: 2 (Character Comedy)
• 5 Panels.

• Panel 1: Total chaos on stage. The crowd scatters, screaming.

• Panel 2: Diego takes one look at the heavily armed Vanguard drones. He immediately stops
playing.

• Diego: "Whoa. Bad vibes. No visa."

• Panel 3: Diego immediately unplugs his red Stratocaster, dives completely behind Phoenix's
massive 4x10 bass cabinet, and curls into a ball, clutching his guitar like a shield.

• Panel 4: Phoenix, completely overwhelmed by the heat, the noise, the missing guitarist, and

hovering uselessly over the bass strings.

• Phoenix (looking around, confused): "My brain thinks we missed a time signature change."

• Panel 5: Focus entirely on Warner. The roof is caving in, lasers are painting the stage, his
bandmates have stopped playing, but Warner does not care. He is sweating, head down,
violently smashing the drums, holding the beat of "Monsters" together by sheer professional
willpower.
