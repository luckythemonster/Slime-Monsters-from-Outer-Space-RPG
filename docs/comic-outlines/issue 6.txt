SLIME MONSTERS FROM OUTER
SPACE: ISSUE #6
Part 1: The Chore Workflows (Pages 1-4)
PAGE 1 – THE STAND-UP (Beat 1) Toon Level: 1 (Corporate dystopia transposed onto a
crappy apartment)
Panel 1 (Wide): Establishing shot of the apartment kitchen. It is deeply, unsettlingly clean. The
scorched blast-crater on the linoleum has been scrubbed so hard it shines. Caption Box (Top
Left): "Day 29 of the Acoustic Embargo." Caption Box (Top Left, below first): "We survived
the audit. We did not survive Phoenix."
Panel 2: Cyan Phoenix stands rigidly at the head of the kitchen island. They are glowing a
pristine, unblinking turquoise. They are holding the clipboard left behind by Intern Unit 74-B.
Phoenix: "Good morning, team. Welcome to our 6:00 AM mandatory stand-up. Let's circle back
on our localized sanitation deliverables."
Panel 3: Reveal the rest of the band. Diego is absurdly tall, wearing bright yellow rubber
dishwashing gloves over his Vanguard corporate shirt. He looks incredibly attentive. Sticky is
quietly polishing a spatula. Diego: "I’ve bandwidth-optimized the drying rack, Phoenix. We are
seeing a 40% increase in ceramic drainage."
Panel 4: Focus on Lucky. They are gripping a wet ceramic plate and a dish towel. Their pink
slime is a hot, jagged, furiously bubbling magenta. They are visibly trembling with the effort of
not screaming.
PAGE 2 – SYNERGIZING THE SPONGE (Beat 1) Toon Level: 1 (Deadpan infuriating)
Panel 1: Phoenix taps the clipboard with a pen. They do not blink. Phoenix: "Excellent metric,
Diego. However, our dish-soaping workflows are currently bottlenecking at the rinse cycle. We
need to proactively pivot our sponge utilization."
Panel 2: Phoenix points directly at Lucky. Phoenix: "Lucky. Your scrubbing metrics are hostile.
We need you to align your rotational friction with Vanguard core competencies. Less
aggression, more synergy."
Panel 3: Close-up on Lucky’s face. A vein of hot magenta is practically leaping off their
forehead. They open their mouth to yell, but suddenly glance up at the Panopticon microphone
on the ceiling, which blinks a faint, warning green.
Panel 4: Lucky snaps their mouth shut. They force a horrifying, strained thumbs-up. Lucky:
(Whispering through clenched, grinding teeth) "Synergy. Right."
PAGE 3 – BOILING POINT (Beat 1) Toon Level: 2 (Exaggerated stress physics)
Panel 1: Lucky turns back to the sink, aggressively drying a coffee mug with the towel.
Panel 2: Close-up on Lucky's hands. The magenta slime is boiling so hot with suppressed rage
that the dish towel begins to singe and smoke.
Panel 3: Lucky moves on to drying a ceramic plate. They are gripping the edges so tightly that
their thumbs are leaving physical indentations in the hard ceramic. SFX: Squeeeeak. (A terrible,
high-pitched friction noise).
Panel 4: The ceramic plate shatters into a dozen pieces in Lucky's hands, entirely crushed by
the sheer physical force of their repressed anger.
PAGE 5 – THE BREAK ROOM (Beat 1/2 Transition) Toon Level: 2 (Claustrophobic)
Panel 1: Phoenix turns to look at the shattered plate. They do not look angry, just disappointed
in a very HR way. Phoenix: "Shattering inventory is a sub-optimal workflow, Lucky. I'm going to
have to write you up for a negative paradigm shift."
Panel 2: Lucky drops the broken pieces of the plate into the sink. They don't even look at
Phoenix. They just turn around and march out of the kitchen, their slime leaving little scorch
marks on the linoleum.
Panel 3: Diego happily picks up a sponge, perfectly filling the gap Lucky left. Diego: "I can
leverage the sweeping synergies to cover the breakage, boss." Phoenix: "Touch base with me
when it's done, Diego."
Panel 4: Lucky arrives at the door to the dog-hair-lined broom closet. They rip the door open
and shove themselves inside, slamming it shut behind them.

Part 2: The HR Grievance (Pages 5-8)
PAGE 5 – THE SANCTUARY (Beat 2) Toon Level: 1 (Gritty, cramped lighting)
Panel 1 (Wide): Inside the stifling, dog-hair and egg-carton-lined broom closet. A single harsh
lightbulb swings. Lucky is sitting on the overturned bucket, glowing furiously.
Panel 2: Pan over slightly. Shannon Crackers is also in the broom closet. She is sitting on a
stack of phone books, wearing reading glasses, and calmly sipping tea from a thermos.
Shannon: "I was wondering when you'd reach your breaking point. The 6:00 AM stand-ups are
a violation of the Geneva Conventions."
Panel 3: Lucky points frantically at the closet door, then at their own throat, then mimes
strangling someone. They are playing furious, silent charades to express their desire to murder
Phoenix.
Panel 4: Shannon nods, perfectly translating the frantic hand gestures. Shannon: "I agree. But
you can't hit them with a guitar. The Panopticon will flag the impact noise. We need an
alternative weapon."
PAGE 6 – THE WEAPON (Beat 2) Toon Level: 1 (Deadpan bureaucracy)
Panel 1: Shannon reaches into her tactical duffel bag. Shannon: "When I was in the Galactic
Navy, I didn't get my commanding officer fired by punching him. I got him fired because he didn't
properly file a Form 104-B for hazardous waste disposal."
Panel 2: Shannon pulls out a massive, terrifyingly thick binder. The cover reads: VANGUARD
HUMAN RESOURCES: BY-LAWS & MANAGERIAL OVERSIGHT (VOLUME IV). Shannon: "If
you want to kill a middle manager..."
Panel 3: Shannon slams the heavy binder onto Lucky’s lap. Dust and dog hair poof into the air.
Shannon: "...You use paperwork."
PAGE 7 – THE LOOPHOLE (Beat 2) Toon Level: 2 (Montage/Frantic pacing)
Panel 1: Montage panel. Lucky and Shannon are hunched over the binder. Shannon is pointing
a pen at a dense paragraph of tiny, unreadable corporate text. Lucky's eyes are wide, absorbing
the bureaucratic dark arts.
Panel 2: Montage panel. Lucky is frantically scribbling on a legal pad with a cheap ballpoint
pen, their tongue sticking out in concentration. The magenta glow is finally starting to cool down
into an engaged, focused pink.
Panel 3: Montage panel. Shannon hands Lucky a piece of paper. Lucky reads it, pumps a fist
silently in the air, and aggressively stamps it with an inkpad.
PAGE 8 – THE DOSSIER (Beat 2) Toon Level: 1 (Dramatic framing)
Panel 1: Close-up on the completed document. It is a 40-page, stapled monstrosity. The cover
page reads: "FORMAL GRIEVANCE: MANAGERIAL OVERREACH & SYNERGY ABUSE.
SUBMITTED BY: LUCKY."
Panel 2: Lucky holds the document up in the harsh light of the closet. They look utterly
exhausted, covered in sweat and Bajonka's dog fur, but there is a dangerous, triumphant gleam
in their eyes.
Panel 3: Shannon packs her thermos away. Shannon: "It's airtight. Cites three separate
violations of localized workflow mandates. It will legally strip Phoenix of their clipboard."
Panel 4: Lucky puts their hand on the doorknob of the closet. They look back at Shannon and
give a single, solemn nod. Caption Box (Bottom Right): "Time to file."
Part 3: The Silent Math War (Pages 9-11)
PAGE 9 – ANOMALOUS DATA (Beat 3) Toon Level: 1 (Tense, grounded framing)
Panel 1 (Wide): Close-up on the bottom of the apartment's front door. The mail slot clicks open.
Through the gap, we see the polished, expensive dress shoes of Steven Alibi standing in the
hallway.
Panel 2: A sleek, heavy, analog drum machine (made of solid osmium) is shoved through the
mail slot. Then another. And another. A dozen of them tumble onto the cheap apartment carpet.
Panel 3: The drum machines hit the floor. Their mechanical metronomes immediately start
sweeping back and forth. Small LED screens on the machines read: "TEMPO: 340 BPM. TIME
SIGNATURE: 11/4. CALIBRATION: OFFENSIVE."
Panel 4: Sticky, who is polishing a spoon at the kitchen sink, suddenly stops. Its rusted head
slowly turns toward the front door. The robot's optical sensors glow a harsh, combat red.
PAGE 10 – 13/8 ASSASSIN (Beat 4) Toon Level: 3 (High-octane martial arts, completely
silent)
Panel 1: The first drum machine's speaker vibrates, a millisecond away from blasting a terrible,
mathematically incorrect bass kick.
Panel 2: Sticky crosses the living room in a blur. It stomps its heavy metal foot directly onto the
machine, crushing the casing flat before a single soundwave can escape.
Panel 3: Another drum machine pops open, deploying tiny cymbals. Sticky lunges, catching the
brass cymbals perfectly between its syrup-coated hands. The syrup acts as an instant acoustic
dampener. SFX: Fwump. (Muffled and sticky).
Panel 4: Sticky is a whirlwind of rusty destruction. It is physically ripping wires out of
motherboards, catching gears in mid-air, and smothering speakers with its sticky chassis. The
robot is moving in a jerky, chaotic 13/8 rhythm, entirely out of sync with the drum machines, but
maintaining absolute, terrifying silence.
PAGE 11 – BLIND SPOT (Beat 5) Toon Level: 1 (Gritty espionage)
Panel 1: While Sticky silently murders drum machines in the background, pan over to the far
corner of the living room, near the window. Bajonka is backed against the wall.
Panel 2: Pete the mop-dog is standing perfectly upright on his hind legs. He reaches a paw up
to his messy fur and brushes it aside, revealing a sleek, glowing Vanguard communication
earpiece.
Panel 3: Close-up on Pete’s face. The oblivious, dumb-dog facade is completely gone. His eyes
are cold and tactical. Caption Box (Pete's Subtitled Barks): "You thought you were the only
operator in this sector."
Panel 4: Bajonka bares her teeth in a silent snarl, shifting her weight to pounce.

PAGE 12 – THE VENDETTA (Beat 5) Toon Level: 1 (Intense, dramatic lore drop)
Panel 1: Pete pulls a tiny, high-tech glass-cutter from his collar and presses it against the
window pane. Caption Box (Pete's Subtitled Barks): "This wasn't about the music. This was
about Laserstein's mansion."
Panel 2: Flashback panel (sepia toned). The billionaire tech-bro gala from Arc 1 collapsing into
the vacuum of space. We see a mop-dog wearing a Vanguard security vest floating away in the
background. Caption Box (Pete's Subtitled Barks): "You blew the dome. You took my
brother."
Panel 3: Back to the present. Pete glares at Bajonka. Caption Box (Pete's Subtitled Barks): "I
took your peace."
PAGE 13 – THE ESCAPE (Beat 6) Toon Level: 2 (Mundane reaction to high action)
Panel 1: The glass-cutter silently pops the window pane loose. Pete catches the glass perfectly,
sets it on the floor, and fires a tiny grappling hook from his collar out into the dystopian Austin
skyline.
Panel 2: Pete salutes Bajonka, then falls backward out the window, repelling into the smog.
Panel 3: Bajonka rushes to the open window. She stands on her hind legs, pointing wildly out
into the city, barking as aggressively as she possibly can without making a single sound (a very
intense pantomime of a bark).
Panel 4: Pull back to show the apartment. Sticky is holding a crushed drum machine. Bajonka is
pointing out the window. Diego walks past both of them, casually inspecting a freshly dried fork.
Diego: "Excellent airflow on the window, Bajonka. Very synergistic."
PAGE 14 – THE FINAL MINUTE (Beat 7) Toon Level: 1 (Tense, claustrophobic pacing)
Panel 1: Extreme close-up on the digital Vanguard Rent Voucher pinned to the wall. The
counter reads: 00:00:45.
Panel 2: The broom closet door kicks open. Lucky marches out. Their pink slime has
transitioned from bubbling magenta to a focused, deadly, hot-pink glow. They are holding the
massive, 40-page HR Grievance.
Panel 3: Cyan Phoenix is standing in the kitchen, checking off boxes on a clipboard. Lucky
walks right up to them and slams the heavy binder directly onto Phoenix’s clipboard. SFX:
THWUMP. (Loud enough to have impact, quiet enough to pass the algorithm).
Panel 4: Phoenix doesn't flinch. Their pristine cyan eyes slowly drag down from Lucky’s face to
the cover of the binder. Phoenix: "Lucky. Unscheduled deliverables are a violation of our core
workflow."

Part 5: Day 30 (Pages 15-18)
PAGE 15 – THE REVIEW (Beat 7/8) Toon Level: 2 (Speed and tension)
Panel 1: Phoenix flips open the binder. Their cyan eyes begin darting back and forth at inhuman
speeds, reading entire pages in milliseconds. Phoenix: "Managerial overreach... unauthorized
workflow delegation... citing Vanguard code 804-A..."
Panel 2: The clock on the wall: 00:00:15.
Panel 3: In the background, Sticky silently twists the head off the final analog drum machine.
Industrial syrup drips onto the carpet.
Panel 4: Phoenix stops on page 34. They point a single, glowing cyan finger at the text.
Phoenix: "Ah. You failed to notarize subsection C. This grievance is algorithmically void."
PAGE 16 – ZERO (Beat 8/9) Toon Level: 3 (Time slowing down)
Panel 1: The clock on the wall reads: 00:00:03.
Panel 2: Lucky doesn't care about the typo. They drop the binder on the floor. With one fluid
motion, they grab the heavy, rusted laser-guitar off the sofa.
Panel 3: The clock on the wall reads: 00:00:01.
Panel 4: Extreme close-up on Lucky's hands. They slam the quarter-inch audio cable directly
into the output jack of the guitar. SFX: Ka-clunk.
Panel 5: The Rent Voucher on the wall flashes. The text changes from RED to bright, glowing
GREEN. Screen Text: EMBARGO LIFTED.
PAGE 17 – CATHARSIS (Beat 9) Toon Level: 3 (Pure, chaotic energy)
Panel 1 (Full Page Splash): The silence breaks. Lucky hits the strings of the laser-guitar with
everything they have. A massive, visually explosive shockwave of jagged, messy soundwaves
erupts from Diego's rusted amplifiers.
The shockwave visibly distorts the air. It blows the remaining paperwork off Phoenix's clipboard.
It knocks Diego's rubber gloves off. It rattles the teeth of everyone in the apartment.
Lucky is arched backward, screaming into the microphone, their slime radiating a brilliant,
triumphant, blinding neon pink. It is the loudest, most abrasive chord ever played.
SFX: KRRRRAAANNNGGGGG!!!!!
PAGE 18 – THE AFTERMATH (Beat 9 / Cliffhanger) Toon Level: 1 (Grounded, ringing ears)
Panel 1: The chord fades out into a low, static hum from the amp. The apartment is completely
trashed again. Paperwork is everywhere. Dead drum machines litter the floor.
Panel 2: Lucky stands there, panting, their slime settling back into a calm, happy pastel pink.
They look around the chaotic room, deeply satisfied.
Panel 3: Focus on Phoenix. The blast of pure, un-synergized noise has visibly rattled them. The
pristine cyan glow flickers, dimming slightly. They reach up and rub their temples. Phoenix: "My
brain thinks..."
Panel 4: Phoenix stops. They look at their own hands, realizing what they just said. The
corporate spell is breaking.
Panel 5: Lucky slings the guitar over their shoulder, stepping over a crushed drum machine.
Lucky: "Meeting adjourned, boss. We've got a rehearsal." Caption Box (Bottom Right): "END
OF ARC 2, PART 2."
