SLIME MONSTERS FROM OUTER
SPACE: ISSUE #5
Part 1: The Mundane Thrivers (Pages 1-4)
PAGE 1 – DAY 14 (Beat 1) Toon Level: 1 (Grounded, mundane dystopia)
Panel 1 (Wide): Establishing shot of the apartment's living room. It’s a cramped, cheap
Vanguard-sector disaster. Cables run everywhere. The scorched crater in the kitchen is visible
in the background. Pinned to the wall is the oversized, glowing Vanguard Corporate Coupon that
reads: "RENT VOUCHER: VALID. ACOUSTIC ANOMALIES: 0." A digital counter below it reads:
"DAY 14 / 30." Caption Box (Top Left): "Day 14 of the Acoustic Embargo." Caption Box (Top
Left, below first): "Halfway home."
Panel 2: Close-up on a rusty, leaking amplifier serving as a makeshift ironing board. A pristine,
crisp white corporate button-down shirt is laid across it. A human hand holding a hot iron
presses down on the collar. SFX: Hsssssss. (Very small, quiet text).
Panel 3: Reveal Diego. He is absurdly tall, utterly unflappable, and currently ironing the
button-down shirt while wearing it over his usual layered, bizarrely stained band clothes. He has
a Vanguard employee badge clipped to a lanyard around his neck. Diego: "The paradigm of
data entry is truly a blessing, Lucky. The spreadsheets are just... digital fields. Waiting to be
harvested by the mind."
Panel 4: Focus on Diego pulling on a terribly fitting corporate tie. He looks at peace. Diego:
"Also, the dental plan covers psychic extractions. Very chill."
PAGE 2 – THE BAKER (Beat 1) Toon Level: 2 (Visual Comedy)
Panel 1 (Wide): Cut to the scorched kitchen. Sticky, the 1950s tin-can robot, is wearing a frilly,
flour-dusted apron over its rusted chassis. Drumsticks are still permanently glued to its hands,
and industrial syrup is actively dripping from its joints. Caption Box (Top Left): "Without music,
some of us have found other ways to express the rhythm."
Panel 2: Sticky is gracefully weaving strands of dough together. Despite the syrup and the
drumsticks, the dough is absolutely immaculate. The robot's mechanical arms are moving in
sharp, jerky, asymmetrical patterns. SFX: Whir-clank. Whir-clank. Squeeeak. (In a distinct 13/8
visual rhythm).
Panel 3: Sticky carefully opens the oven door (which only has one hinge left from Bajonka's
explosion last issue).
Panel 4: Sticky presents a perfectly baked, golden, braided challah bread on a rusty baking
sheet. It holds it up like a religious artifact. A single drop of industrial robot-syrup falls onto the
floor next to it. Sticky: (No dialogue, just a satisfied sequence of silent blinking lights).
PAGE 3 – MACH 2 (Beat 1) Toon Level: 2 (Exaggerated Physics)
Panel 1: Cut to the living room floor. A pile of at least twenty crushed, empty Vanguard-brand
espresso cups.
Panel 2: Reveal Lucky. They look completely unhinged. Their neon pink slime is boiling a deep,
jagged, furious magenta. They are physically vibrating at Mach 2, leaving motion-blur outlines
around their body. Lucky: (Muttering, text shaky and small) "Just gotta keep the hands busy.
Idle hands play power chords. Power chords equal homelessness. Keep the hands busy."
Panel 3: Close up on Lucky's hands. They are holding a tiny jeweler's screwdriver, aggressively
trying to force a screw into the side of a completely disassembled, cheap plastic toaster. Their
slime is bubbling around the screwdriver handle.
Panel 4: The screwdriver slips, scratching loudly against the toaster casing. SFX: SKRRRCH!
Panel 5: Lucky freezes. Their vibrating stops instantly. Their magenta eyes widen in absolute,
paralyzing terror. They stare at the glowing Vanguard Panopticon microphone on the ceiling.
PAGE 4 – INTERNAL SCREAMING (Beat 1) Toon Level: 1 (Emotional focus)
Panel 1: The Panopticon microphone blinks green once. It doesn't register the scrape as music.
Lucky exhales, their body melting slightly into a puddle of relief. The magenta cools down just a
fraction.
Panel 2: Diego steps into frame, holding a slice of Sticky's immaculate challah bread. He takes
a bite. Diego: "You should try baking, Lucky. It really aligns the chakras. Sticky says the yeast is
screaming, but in a good way."
Panel 3: Over-the-shoulder shot from Diego's perspective, looking down at Lucky sitting in the
pile of toaster parts and coffee cups. Lucky forces a horrifyingly wide, manic smile. They are
trying so hard to be a supportive friend, but their slime is practically sparking with resentment.
Lucky: "I am so happy for you guys. I truly am."
Panel 4: Extreme close-up on Lucky's face. The smile is cracking. A vein of hot magenta pulses
on their forehead. Lucky: "I love that you are thriving in the algorithm. I love that I am the only
one whose soul is currently suffocating in a beige, synergistic void."
Panel 5: Diego pats Lucky gently on the head, oblivious to the existential crisis. Sticky whirs
happily in the background. Diego: "See? Synergy. We're synergizing." Lucky: (Whispering,
gritting teeth) "I'm going to eat this toaster."

PAGE 5 – THE COLD WAR (Beat 2) Toon Level: 1 (Grounded, gritty espionage tone)
Panel 1 (Wide): Extreme low angle, right at floor level, under the cheap sofa. The lighting is
intensely dramatic, like a gritty spy thriller. Bajonka is belly-crawling across the carpet. She is
wearing a custom-tailored, black tactical turtleneck. Caption Box (Top Left): "At the floor level,
the silence isn't a retreat. It's a theater of war."
Panel 2: Point-of-view from Bajonka's perspective, looking through a pair of tiny thermal
binoculars. The crosshairs are locked onto Pete, the mop-dog. Pete is just sitting in the middle
of the living room, staring blankly at a blank wall.
Panel 3: Close-up on Bajonka’s paw. She uses a single claw to slowly, silently slide a manila
folder across the carpet toward Pete. The folder is stamped with the Vanguard Red Star logo.
Panel 4: Focus on the folder as it stops right in front of Pete's paws. We can read the tab:
"CLASSIFIED DOSSIER: SLEEPER AGENT P.E.T.E." Paperclips hold high-contrast
surveillance photos of Pete interacting with corporate drones.
Panel 5: Bajonka narrows her eyes, waiting for the enemy agent to break cover and react to the
intercepted intel.
PAGE 6 – ASYMMETRIC WARFARE (Beat 2) Toon Level: 1 (Deadpan mundane reaction)
Panel 1: Pete looks down at the highly classified Vanguard dossier.
Panel 2: Pete slowly licks a piece of fuzzy gray lint off the carpet right next to the folder. He
does not acknowledge the documents at all.
Panel 3: Bajonka looks directly at the "camera" (breaking the fourth wall slightly) with an
expression of chilling, absolute disgust.
Panel 4 (Tall Panel): Shannon Crackers walks right through the middle of the "warzone." She’s
wearing a faded Joni Mitchell t-shirt and holding a mug of tea. She steps directly over the
classified dossier without looking down. Shannon: "I gotta say, I am loving this acoustic
embargo. Finally, I can hear myself think without that 13/8 metallic racket."
Panel 5: Shannon stops, looking down at the carpet. Shannon: "Lucky, what are you doing?"
PAGE 7 – THE HARVEST (Beat 2/3 Transition) Toon Level: 2 (Exaggerated, pathetic
desperation)
Panel 1: Pull back to reveal Lucky on their hands and knees, just a few feet away from
Shannon. Lucky’s pink slime is still a hot, jagged magenta. They are furiously dragging a sticky
lint roller across the carpet, collecting massive clumps of Bajonka’s shed Shiba fur. Lucky:
"Harvesting."
Panel 2: Shannon takes a sip of her tea, entirely unimpressed by the frantic alien on the floor.
Shannon: "You're harvesting dog fur from the rug." Lucky: "Biomass, Shannon. It's all about
the biomass. High tensile strength. Maximum sound dampening. You wouldn't understand. You
aren't an artist."
Panel 3: Lucky aggressively rips a sheet of fur-covered tape off the lint roller. They clutch the
clump of fur to their chest like it's gold. Lucky: "I have to go to my sanctuary now. The toaster
needs me."
Panel 4: Lucky scurries down the narrow hallway toward a door slightly ajar at the end.
Shannon watches them go, completely deadpan. Shannon: "Okay. Just don't bleed any of that
pink stuff on the security deposit."
PAGE 8 – THE SANCTUARY (Beat 3) Toon Level: 2 (Claustrophobic, grimy aesthetic)
Panel 1 (Full Page Splash): A cross-section view of the broom closet. It is agonizingly small,
barely wider than Lucky's shoulders.
The walls are an absolute nightmare of cheap, DIY soundproofing. It's a collage of taped-up
cardboard egg cartons, stolen couch cushions, and massive, gross patches of Bajonka's shed
fur held up by hardened, crusty patches of Lucky’s own pink slime.
A single, harsh yellow lightbulb dangles from the ceiling.
Lucky is sitting cross-legged on an overturned plastic bucket in the center. They are hunched
over, sweating profusely in the stifling heat, holding the disassembled toaster on their lap. They
look utterly pathetic, stripped of all their usual abrasive bravado.
Caption Box (Bottom Left): "They took the noise." Caption Box (Bottom Right): "And left me
with nothing but the acoustics of my own failure."

PAGE 9 – THE FATAL HUM (Beat 4) Toon Level: 1 (Tight, tense, mundane focus)
Panel 1: Extreme close-up on the toaster mechanism inside the cramped, dog-hair-lined broom
closet. Lucky’s hands (still glowing a stressed magenta) finally slide the tiny metal catch into
place. SFX: Click.
Panel 2: Close-up on Lucky's face in the harsh yellow light. The intense, vibrating magenta
dims just a fraction into a softer pink. A look of genuine, innocent satisfaction washes over their
face. For one split second, they forget the embargo.
Panel 3: Lucky wipes a bead of sweat from their forehead with the back of their hand. Their
eyes are closed. Lucky: (Small, musical notes floating in the air) "Hmm-hmm..."
Panel 4: Silence. The musical notes hang in the air, perfectly drawn, floating toward the ceiling.
Lucky’s eyes snap open, the pupils shrinking to tiny dots. Total realization.
Panel 5: A red laser grid instantly scans down from the ceiling of the broom closet.
PAGE 10 – THE PANOPTICON WAKES (Beat 4) Toon Level: 1 (Bureaucratic Horror)
Panel 1 (Wide): Cut back to the living room. Everything is still peaceful. Diego is admiring his
ironed shirt; Sticky is setting the challah bread on a cooling rack; Pete is still staring at the wall
while Bajonka glares at him.
Panel 2: Suddenly, every single piece of Vanguard-branded technology in the apartment turns a
harsh, glowing red. The toaster on the counter, Diego's corporate employee badge, the
thermostat, and the giant Rent Voucher pinned to the wall.
Panel 3: Close-up on the digital counter on the wall. The text violently changes. Screen Text:
DAY 14 / 30. Screen Text (Flickering to): ANOMALY DETECTED. AUDIT PENDING.
Panel 4: A sterilized, perfectly pleasant, synthesized female voice echoes from the ceiling
microphone. Ceiling Speaker (Vanguard AI): "Acoustic Anomaly registered in Sector 4. A
Vanguard Human Resources representative has been dispatched for a random compliance
audit."
Panel 5: Diego pauses, holding a piece of challah bread halfway to his mouth. He looks mildly
inconvenienced. Diego: "Ah. Bad vibes."
PAGE 11 – THE PUDDLE (Beat 5) Toon Level: 3 (Extreme cartoon physics / Slime
anatomy)
Panel 1: Cut back to the bottom of the closed broom closet door. A single, pathetic, sickly
pastel-pink droplet of slime oozes out from the crack underneath.
Panel 2: The droplet is followed by a rush of liquid. Lucky has completely lost physical
cohesion. They are physically melting under the door, pouring out into the hallway as a mass of
depressed, bubbling, pastel-colored goop.
Panel 3: The puddle of Lucky slides down the hallway and pools onto the living room rug. Two
miserable, sunken eyes and a wobbly mouth form on the surface of the puddle.
Panel 4: Phoenix, still gray and sweating profusely, pushes their slipping glasses up their nose.
They stare down at the pathetic pink puddle ruining the cheap carpet. Phoenix: "My brain thinks
you hummed."
PAGE 12 – THE CONFESSION (Beat 5) Toon Level: 3 (Melodrama)
Panel 1: The Lucky-puddle spreads out further, reaching toward Diego’s shoes. Lucky (Puddle
Form): (Gurgling text, wavy bubble) "I am a failure. I am a loud, useless void. I couldn't even fix
the toaster without making it a musical performance."
Panel 2: Sticky slowly walks over, whirring in 13/8 time, and gently places a perfectly baked,
silent croissant directly into the center of the Lucky-puddle. It slowly sinks into their slime.
Panel 3: The Lucky-puddle bubbles with pathetic, self-loathing tears. Lucky (Puddle Form):
"Look at Sticky. An industrial robot covered in syrup, baking French pastry in total silence. Look
at Diego! He has a 401k now! And I am just a puddle of contraband audio!"
Panel 4: Phoenix’s hands ball into fists. The sweat on their forehead is starting to steam.
Phoenix: "Lucky..."
Panel 5: Close-up on Phoenix. The thick glasses crack slightly down the middle. A faint, cyan
glow begins to emanate from their gray slime. Phoenix: "Get up. My brain thinks... my brain
thinks I am absolutely done with this."

PAGE 13 – THE MATH (Beat 6) Toon Level: 2 (Exaggerated stress)
Panel 1: Focus on Phoenix, still gray, sweating buckets. Their thick glasses are completely
fogged up. They are staring down at the pathetic Lucky-puddle on the floor. Phoenix: "My brain
thinks you don't understand the gravity of this hum, Lucky."
Panel 2: Phoenix grabs a discarded Vanguard espresso cup from Lucky’s pile and begins
furiously scribbling equations on the side of it with a marker. Phoenix: "If the Panopticon voids
the coupon, the base rent reinstates at 4,500 credits. Plus the 18.5% algorithmic anomaly
penalty..."
Panel 3: Close-up on Phoenix's face. The sweat is physically steaming off their head. The gray
slime is starting to bubble. Phoenix: "...Compounded hourly over 14 days, accounting for the
Vanguard convenience tax, we instantly owe exactly 142,876.43 credits!"
Panel 4: Phoenix throws the cup at the wall. It shatters. Phoenix: "MY BRAIN THINKS WE
ARE FINANCIALLY LIQUIDATED!"
PAGE 14 – THE PARADIGM SHIFT (Beat 6) Toon Level: 3 (Magical realism /
Transformation)
Panel 1 (Wide): The sheer terror of the math reaches a critical mass. A blinding, immaculate
cyan light bursts from Phoenix's body, illuminating the entire dingy living room. Diego shields his
eyes. The Lucky-puddle recoils.
Panel 2: Phoenix stands up straight. The thick, foggy glasses slide off their face and shatter on
the floor. They don't need them anymore.
Panel 3: Close-up on Phoenix. They are no longer a sweaty, dull gray puddle of anxiety. They
are a perfectly smooth, pristine, glowing turquoise hydro-gel. Not a single drop of sweat
remains. Their eyes are wide, unblinking, and terrifyingly calm.
Panel 4: Diego looks impressed, taking another bite of challah bread. Diego: "Whoa. Total
chakra alignment. You look like expensive mouthwash." Phoenix: "I am optimized."
PAGE 15 – PERFECT RHYTHM (Beat 7) Toon Level: 1 (Deadpan coolness)
Panel 1: The Lucky-puddle bubbles, a tiny pink slime-hand reaching up in confusion. In doing
so, it bumps the edge of the coffee table, knocking off a heavy, rusted crescent wrench. Lucky
(Puddle Form): (Wobbly text) "Phoenix... you didn't say 'my brain thinks'..."
Panel 2: The wrench plummets toward the floor.
Panel 3: Without even looking down, Cyan Phoenix snaps their hand out and catches the heavy
wrench perfectly by the handle. A small, visual shockwave of tempo radiates from the catch.
SFX: THWACK. (Perfectly aligned with the panel border).
Panel 4: Phoenix turns to the band. They are moving with eerie, metronomic precision.
Phoenix: "The anxiety was simply unprocessed data. I see the algorithm now. I see the rhythm
of the Vanguard's bureaucracy. I am operating on absolute certainty."
PAGE 16 – THE AUDIT ARRIVES (Beat 8) Toon Level: 2 (Frantic pacing)
Panel 1: A sharp, aggressive knock at the door. Three perfectly spaced beats. SFX: BAM. BAM.
BAM.
Panel 2: Through the peephole view. Standing in the hallway is a human in a crisp Vanguard
polo shirt, holding a clipboard. He is moving with stiff, 90-degree robotic jerks, desperately
pretending to be a machine. His name tag reads "INTERN UNIT 74-B (KEVIN)".
Panel 3: Chaos in the apartment. Diego is frantically shoving Sticky the robot into the
dog-hair-lined broom closet. Sticky is physically incapable of stopping its chaotic drumming
motion. Sticky: Whir-clank. Squeak. Whir-clank. Diego: "Get in the biomass, Sticky! Feel the
fur!"
Panel 4: Cyan Phoenix calmly walks past the panicked Diego and the shivering Lucky-puddle,
approaching the front door. Phoenix straightens an invisible tie. Phoenix: "Hold my calls. I will
handle the audit."

Part 5: The HR Audit (Pages 17-20)

ID and surrender your lease deliverables. Vzzt."
Panel 2: Phoenix stares back, unblinking, glowing a bright, intimidating cyan.
Panel 3: Intern Unit 74-B sweats slightly under the intense, glowing stare. He breaks character
for half a second. Intern Unit 74-B (Kevin): (Normal, nervous voice) "Uh, I mean... beep boop. I
am here to evict you."
PAGE 18 – WEAPONIZED SYNERGY (Beat 9) Toon Level: 1 (Hypnotic, deadpan dialogue)
Panel 1: Phoenix leans into the doorway, looming over the intern. Phoenix: "Negative, Intern
Unit. You have misread the localized paradigm. There was no anomaly."
Panel 2: Focus on Phoenix’s face. They begin delivering corporate jargon with the rhythmic,
terrifying cadence of a master spellcaster. Phoenix: "We were proactively beta-testing a
disruptive audio-wellness initiative to maximize tenant-retention bandwidth."
Panel 3: Intern Unit 74-B blinks, his clipboard lowering slightly. His human brain is struggling to
process the sheer density of the HR buzzwords. Intern Unit 74-B (Kevin): "Vzzt... Processing
bandwidth?"
PAGE 19 – THE OVERWRITE (Beat 9) Toon Level: 2 (Visualizing the jargon)
Panel 1: Phoenix takes a step out into the hallway, backing the intern up. Floating, holographic
Vanguard buzzwords ("SYNERGY," "PARADIGM," "DELIVERABLES") seem to manifest in the
air around Phoenix’s glowing cyan form. Phoenix: "Exactly. We are synergizing our holistic
silence deliverables to align with Vanguard core competencies. Writing us up would negatively
impact your quarterly synergy metrics, Kevin."
Panel 2: The intern drops his pen. The use of his real name, combined with the threat to his
synergy metrics, completely short-circuits his fake-robot persona. Intern Unit 74-B (Kevin):
"My... my metrics?"
Panel 3: Phoenix places a single, perfectly smooth cyan hand on the intern's shoulder.
Phoenix: "Close the ticket, Kevin. Let's touch base offline in Q4. Pivot."
