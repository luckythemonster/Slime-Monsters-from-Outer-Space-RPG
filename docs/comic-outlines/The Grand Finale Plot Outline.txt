SLIME MONSTERS FROM OUTER
SPACE
Final Issue: The Elephant in the Room (Grand Finale)
PLOT SUMMARY
Part 1: The Luthier of Debris Stranded in a field of planetary wreckage, the band prepares to
hunt down Jeffrey Laserstein. But Lucky is weaponless. They undertake a dangerous scavenger
run through the floating debris of a destroyed Vanguard armory. Sticky, leaking syrup and
sparking, desperately welds a new instrument together: osmium neck, plasma-jet strings,
laser-bomb pickups, and the rusted hubcap of their old space-van as the body. While Sticky
builds, the band is attacked by Vanguard drones. Having no guitar, Lucky weaponizes their
voice. They scream Vanguard's own "Terms & Conditions" at the drones, their word balloons
manifesting as massive, jagged blocks of heavy pink energy that physically crush the attackers.
Part 2: The "Epstein" Protocol Armed with the ultimate junk-heavy laser-guitar, Bajonka leads
a highly tactical, military-grade infiltration into a Vanguard Maximum Security Space-Prison to
confront Laserstein. They breach his heavily fortified cell, only to find the door wide open and
Laserstein dead on the floor (an obvious, staged "suicide"). A golden holographic projector
flickers to life, revealing Darnell Trunk—the richest elephant in the universe. Trunk smugly
admits he had Laserstein killed to cover up his own corporate crimes and challenges the band
to face him on his hyper-yacht.
Part 3: The Golden Throne (Boss Phases 1 & 2) The band breaches Trunk’s obscenely
luxurious hyper-yacht. Trunk initiates Phase 1 of the boss fight by erecting a literal, physical
"Paywall" across the battlefield. Sticky goes into overdrive, pounding out a chaotic 13/8
math-rock beat that shatters the paywall's algorithmic bricks. In Phase 2, Trunk retreats to a
massive golden space-toilet. As he sits there, he launches a barrage of glowing, predatory
NDAs and loans (which Diego casually deflects with chill, low-end bass frequencies) and begins
tweeting. The tweets manifest as disgusting, boorish blue robotic birds that swoop down,
physically screaming the misspelled text at the band.
Part 4: The Fourth-Wall Break The battle becomes a chaotic mess of screaming birds and
golden toilet attacks. Suddenly, Lucky stops playing. They drop their newly forged
hubcap-guitar, exhausted and deeply offended. Leaning into the microphone, Lucky breaks the
fourth wall, looking directly at the reader to complain about how incredibly lazy and "first-year
improv" it is to make the villain a greedy, orange elephant. The sheer narrative critique shatters
the illusion of the comic. The elephant facade drops. The art style violently shifts, revealing
Trunk as a grotesque, hyper-realistic collage of cutout Donald Trump photos. Unfazed by the
jarring realism, Lucky reaches into the panel, physically rolls the cutout collage into a massive
joint, and smokes him, destroying the billionaire once and for all.
Part 5: Artisanal Anti-Matter (Denouement) The universe is saved. The band commandeers
Trunk’s trillion-dollar rocket-yacht, mounting the actual elephant head (complete with cartoon X's
over its eyes) to the hood ornament like a morbid trophy. They punch the throttle to fly off into
the cosmos. However, as they sit in the luxurious, gold-plated cockpit, Phoenix begins sweating
profusely. Checking the dashboard, Phoenix realizes the hyper-yacht runs exclusively on
"Artisanal Anti-Matter"—which costs more than their collective lives. Despite having saved the
galaxy and stolen a billionaire's flagship, they are still completely broke. Diego unplugs the
yacht's right-wing talk radio, Lucky plugs in their guitar, and they desperately set coordinates for
their next gig just to pay for gas.

NUMBERED BEAT MAP
Beat 1 (Toon Level: 2) Floating in space debris. Sticky is violently welding a hubcap to an
osmium pole using plasma jets. A Vanguard security drone approaches. Lucky inhales deeply
and screams, "VOID WHERE PROHIBITED BY LAW!" The text physically solidifies into a
jagged, neon pink block that swats the drone out of the air.
Beat 2 (Toon Level: 1) The Prison Break. Gritty, tactical espionage. Bajonka drops from an air
vent into Laserstein's cell. The band follows. Laserstein is dead on the floor. A Vanguard guard
is visible through the window, suspiciously whistling and looking the other way.
Beat 3 (Toon Level: 2) A golden hologram of Darnell Trunk (a massive, orange elephant in an
ill-fitting suit) appears in the cell. He delivers a smug monologue about cutting overhead costs
(murder). Lucky grips the new hubcap-guitar, their slime boiling magenta.
Beat 4 (Toon Level: 3) The Boss Fight: Phase 1. Aboard the hyper-yacht. Trunk drops a
massive, glowing brick "Paywall." Sticky's mechanical arms blur in chaotic 13/8 time. The sheer,
irregular math-rock time signature creates localized earthquakes that shatter the bricks.
Beat 5 (Toon Level: 3) The Boss Fight: Phase 2. Trunk is sitting on a golden space-toilet.
Disgusting blue birds fly from his phone, screaming terrible text like "SAD!" and "LOSER
SLIME!" Diego, completely unflappable, slaps a bassline that creates a protective sonic bubble,
bouncing glowing "NDA" contracts into the walls.
Beat 6 (Toon Level: Meta) Lucky stops playing. The music cuts out. Lucky looks directly at the
"camera" with a look of utter disgust. Lucky: "An orange elephant? Really? We lost our
apartment for a first-year improv level allegory? This is embarrassing for everyone involved."
Beat 7 (Jarring Art-Style Shift) Lucky's critique physically breaks the comic. The Toon Level 3
elephant shatters like glass. In his place is a terrifying, hyper-realistic, Dadaist collage of Donald
Trump photos. The background of the panel becomes blank white space.
Beat 8 (Toon Level: 3) Lucky's cartoon hand reaches into the hyper-realistic collage. They
aggressively roll the cutout photos into a massive, paper joint. Lucky lights it with the plasma
strings of their guitar, takes a drag, and blows a cloud of pink smoke. The boss is defeated.
Beat 9 (Toon Level: 2) Exterior shot of the rocket-yacht flying through space. The decapitated
elephant head is strapped to the hood with bungee cords, cartoon X's on its eyes.
Beat 10 (Toon Level: 1) Inside the cockpit. The ultimate mundane dystopia. Phoenix is staring
at the fuel gauge, melting into a puddle of absolute, sweaty despair. Phoenix: "My brain thinks...
my brain thinks it's a hundred thousand credits per gallon." Diego: (Tuning his bass) "We better
sell some merch at this next one, man." Caption Box (Bottom Right): "THE END. (FOR NOW.)"
