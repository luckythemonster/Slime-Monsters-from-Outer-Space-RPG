  ●​ 4 Panels.

     ARCHIVES & NOISE SHOWS.
  ●​ Panel 2: Interior. Towering, dusty bookshelves have been aggressively shoved against
     the walls to create a makeshift mosh pit. Punks, aliens, and Vanguard dropouts are milling
     around reading corners.
  ●​ Panel 3: At the front circulation desk sits the Terrifying Librarian. She is an older human
     woman with severe cat-eye glasses, a tight bun, and a heavy studded leather jacket over
     a cardigan. She is stamping a book with violent force.
  ●​ Panel 4: Lucky and Phoenix are standing near the stage. Phoenix looks miserable.
        ○​ Lucky: "I kind of love this room. The acoustics smell like old paper."
        ○​ Phoenix: "My brain thinks we are going to have to pay for any books that get bled
            on."

PAGE 4 – THE FOIL (Beat 4)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Lucky walks over to Steven Alibi, trying to project casual punk confidence.
        ○​ Lucky: "Hey, Steven. Huge fan of your analog drum machine rig on the Rust & Bile
             EP. I'm Lucky. We're the Slime Monsters, we're opening right before you."
  ●​ Panel 2: Alibi doesn't even look at Lucky. He pops the latches on his heavy case,
     revealing his signature guitar. It is forged from solid, dull-gray osmium.
        ○​ Alibi: "I know who you are. The biological anomaly band."
  ●​ Panel 3: Alibi finally looks up, adjusting his glasses. His gaze is completely devoid of
     warmth.
        ○​ Alibi: "Your guitarist relies entirely too much on fuzz to cover his sloppy phrasing,
             your bassist plays behind the beat, and you treat a gig like a therapy session
             instead of an audio engineering task."
  ●​ Panel 4: Lucky completely freezes. The neon pink slime instantly bristles, turning
     defensive and slightly jagged. It’s a direct hit to their deepest insecurities.

  ●​ Toon Level: 1

        ○​ Lucky: "Punk isn't about perfectly quantized drum machines, Alibi. It's about raw

        ○​ Alibi: "Try not to bleed on the monitors when you have your tantrum tonight. They're

  ●​ Panel 4: Lucky is left standing alone in the stacks, glowing a boiling, furious, insecure

  ●​ Caption Box (Bottom Corner): "Next: Telepathic Starch."

PAGE 15 – THE ARGUMENT (Beat 1)

• Toon Level: 2 (Surreal Comedy)

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Diego sprints into the "Young Adult" section. The merch table is untouched. Bajonka
is still guarding the empty cashbox, her eyes tracking Diego suspiciously.

• Panel 2: Diego leans over the table. The telepathic chatter from the Sharpie-drawn potatoes is
getting louder, represented by overlapping, jagged green speech balloons.

• Potato: "We are all slime."

• Potato: "The Sharpie ink burns our porous skin."

• Potato: "Rice is the superior side dish."

• Panel 3: Diego crosses his arms, looking genuinely offended on behalf of the potatoes.

• Diego: "Okay, look, I hear your trauma about the ink, but you guys really need to stop
internalizing this anti-tuber propaganda! Rice is a filler. You have potassium!"

• Panel 4: Diego starts gesturing wildly, arguing loudly with the vegetable.
• Diego: "You can be mashed! You can be fried! You are versatile! Stop letting the Vanguard
dictate your culinary self-worth!"

PAGE 16 – THE INCIDENT (Beat 2)

• Toon Level: 2 (Physical Comedy)

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Diego is so heated in his argument that he throws his hands up in exasperation.
WHACK. The headstock of his red Stratocaster catches the edge of the cheap folding table.

• Panel 2 (Large Action): The table completely flips over. The cashbox clatters to the floor.
Dozens of glowing, Sharpie-drawn cosmic potatoes go flying through the air, scattering in every
direction across the library floor.

• Panel 3: Diego gasps, hands on his head.

• Diego: "Oh no! The merch! I'm sorry, little guys! The universe is chaotic!"

• Panel 4: One single potato, glowing faintly and bearing the crude triangle slime logo, rolls
across the floor and bumps gently into a heavy, black leather boot.

PAGE 17 – THE CLINICIAN RETURNS (Beat 2 Cont.)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Slowly pan up from the heavy leather boot. It's Steven Alibi.

• Panel 2: Alibi is standing in the middle of the aisle, carrying two massive, heavy analog drum
machines. He looks down at the crude, Sharpie-drawn potato touching his boot with absolute,
unbridled disgust.

• Panel 3: Diego is on his hands and knees, frantically trying to scoop up the scattered potatoes.
Bajonka is sitting nearby, watching the disaster unfold with a look of quiet canine resignation.

• Panel 4: Lucky and Phoenix walk into the aisle, having heard the crash. They freeze, taking in
the scene: the flipped table, Diego arguing with vegetables on the floor, and Steven Alibi staring
at them like they are a disease.

PAGE 18 – THE PUT-DOWN (Beat 3)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 1: Alibi sets his heavy drum machines down. He looks at Lucky. His voice is cold, quiet,
and perfectly measured.

• Alibi: "You bring stolen agricultural waste into a venue. You draw on it with markers. Your
guitarist is having a psychotic break on the floor."

• Panel 2: Close-up on Alibi's face.

• Alibi: "You don't care about the craft. You just want an excuse to be loud. This is a joke."

• Panel 3: Lucky's pink slime completely deflates, turning a dull, bruised purple. Phoenix looks
down at the floor, their gray slime pooling heavily. The insult lands with devastating accuracy.

• Panel 4: Alibi turns and walks away toward the stage to set up his rig, stepping directly over a
potato without breaking stride.

• Alibi: "Get this garbage out of my way before I start my set."

PAGE 19 – ROCK BOTTOM (Beat 3 Cont.)

• Toon Level: 1

• 4 Panels.

• Panel 2: Diego stands up, dusting off his knees, holding a handful of potatoes. He looks at

• Diego: "Hey man, don't listen to him. His aura is entirely focused on output instead of process.
Also, I think we lost one under the sci-fi shelves."

• Panel 3: Lucky just shakes their head, looking exhausted. The fight has completely left them.

• Lucky: "Just... just pack it up, Diego. We're done."

• Panel 4: Phoenix holds up their phone. The screen glows brightly. EVICTION FINALIZED:
PLEASE VACATE.

• Phoenix (deadpan): "My brain thinks we are officially unhoused."

PAGE 23 – EMPATHY (Beat 4)

• Toon Level: 1

• 5 Panels.

• Panel 1: Diego stops mid-bite of his potato, looking horrified.

• Diego: "Whoa. That is the darkest possible vibe, man. Cannibalism is fundamentally un-chill."

• Panel 2: The promoter holds out the clipboard and a pen toward Lucky.

• Promoter: "We need your band's specific brand of 'acoustic destruction' to breach the

shoves it back into his chest.

• Lucky: "Fine. Where's the mansion?"
