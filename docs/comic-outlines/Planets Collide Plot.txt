Bonus Issue: Planets Collide (Mid-Game
Cliffhanger)
Logline: The 30-day silent retreat is over, but before the band can celebrate, billionaire Jeffrey
Laserstein manipulates a rusted iron golem into playing a sludge-metal riff so dense it pulls two
planets into a catastrophic collision. The band must survive an unwinnable musical boss fight
that literally shatters their world.

I. PLOT OUTLINE
Part 1: The Gravitational Shift The 30-day gag order has just expired. The band is finally
plugging in at their apartment when the gravity suddenly shifts, pulling their amps toward the
ceiling. In the sky above, a second planet is visibly, rapidly approaching. The cause is Kurt
Breezestein—a massive, rusted iron golem resembling a bearded heavy-metal titan—standing
on a nearby Vanguard skyscraper. He is playing a sludge riff so physically dense and
mathematically slow that it generates its own gravitational pull. Watching from a hovering luxury
yacht is Jeffrey Laserstein, who manipulated Kurt into believing the inhabitants of these planets
are evil and that God wants them destroyed (conveniently crushing the Vanguard server farms
holding evidence of Laserstein's crimes).
Part 2: The Unwinnable Boss Fight The Slime Monsters drag their gear to the rooftop to
counter the frequency. Diego is deeply, spiritually impressed by the sheer magnitude of Kurt’s
low-end tone. The band initiates a "Sludge Duel." However, Sticky the robot physically struggles
to play the agonizingly slow tempo; its rusted gears violently grind as it tries to force its 13/8
math-rock programming to wait fifteen minutes between snare hits. The player assumes control
of Lucky for what appears to be a standard boss fight, desperately hitting the "Heavier Riff"
inputs to push the approaching planet back.
Part 3: The Fake-Out (End of the World) No matter how perfectly the player performs, Kurt's
gravity is too strong. The boss fight is scripted to be unwinnable. Laserstein's yacht jumps to
hyperspace. The two planets breach each other's atmospheres. The sky turns into a blinding
wall of fire and tectonic friction. Lucky hits one final, desperate chord on the laser guitar just as
the planet cracks in half. The screen cuts abruptly to black. No music. No sound. Just the words:
INTERMISSION. The player is led to believe they failed and the game is over.
Part 4: The Escape Pod & The Missing Guitar Fade in post-intermission. The camera pans
across a silent, floating debris field of planetary chunks. On one barren asteroid sits the band's
battered apartment couch and Phoenix's keyboard. Bajonka managed to hotwire a Vanguard
emergency escape pod at the exact moment of impact. The band is alive, gasping for air inside
the pod's limited atmospheric bubble. However, as they take inventory, a horrifying realization
sets in: Bajonka couldn't save everything. Lucky's heavy laser guitar—their primary weapon and
instrument—was lost in the planet's destruction.
Part 5: Stepping Up Phoenix looks out at the dust cloud that used to be their Vanguard housing
sector and completely melts into a devastated, gray puddle on the surviving couch. Not only did
they lose their home, but there is absolutely no way the landlord is returning the security deposit
now. The band is stranded, weaponless, and homeless. But instead of raging, Lucky's neon pink
slime solidifies. Stripped of their guitar, Lucky steps up, taking command of the surviving crew to
figure out a way off this rock.

II. BEAT MAP
Part 1: The Sky is Falling (Pages 1-5)
  ●​ Beat 1: Day 31. The apartment. The embargo is lifted. Diego clicks his amp on. Before he
     plucks a string, the gravity tilts 45 degrees. The coffee table slides into the wall. (Toon
     Level: 1)
  ●​ Beat 2: The Golem. The band rushes to the roof. Standing on a massive Vanguard tower
     is Kurt Breezestein, a colossal iron golem. He strikes a single, impossibly slow chord. The
     shockwave visibly distorts the air. (Toon Level: 2)
  ●​ Beat 3: The Mastermind. Jeffrey Laserstein's yacht hovers nearby. Laserstein
     broadcasts his smug, Epstein-esque justification: clearing "corrupt" real estate while
     simultaneously erasing his own legal liabilities. (Toon Level: 1)

Part 2: The Sludge Duel (Pages 6-12 / Gameplay Section)
  ●​ Beat 1: The Counter-Frequency. The band sets up on the roof. Diego’s eyes are wide
     with reverence as the rusted iron golem's bass frequencies rattle his internal organs.
     (Toon Level: 1)
  ●​ Beat 2: Sticky's Breakdown. Sticky tries to lay down a sludge beat. Sparks fly from the
     tin-can chassis as the robot actively fights its own coding to play that slowly. It looks like it
     is in physical pain. (Toon Level: 2)
  ●​ Beat 3: The Boss Fight. (Player Phase). Lucky unleashes raw magenta slime energy
     into the laser guitar, trying to push the other planet back. The UI shows the player dealing
     massive damage, but Kurt's "health bar" doesn't even flinch.

Part 3: Annihilation (Pages 13-16 / Cutscene)
  ●​ Beat 1: Escape. Laserstein sips a glowing drink on his yacht, hits the hyper-drive, and
     vanishes.
  ●​ Beat 2: The Impact. Kurt Breezestein hits the final, devastating down-stroke. The second
     planet fills the entire sky. The crust of the earth violently splinters beneath the band's feet.
     (Toon Level: 3)
  ●​ Beat 3: Fade to Black. Lucky screams, playing one last defiant chord as a wall of fire
     consumes the rooftop. Instant cut to black. Absolute silence.
  ●​ Beat 4: INTERMISSION title card appears.

  ●​ Beat 3: The Toll. The band crawls out of the pod. They are alive. Lucky frantically
