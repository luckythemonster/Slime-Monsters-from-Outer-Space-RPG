PAGE 1 – SPLASH: THE ESCAPE FROM PLANET CALIFORNIA
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ Panel 1 (Full Page Splash): A massive, brutally sleek corporate star-cruiser looms in
     orbit over "Planet California"—a gentrified, hyper-commercialized nightmare world
     encased in glowing digital billboards.
  ●​ Focus: Deep inside the ship’s drop-pod bay, we see Lucky. Right now, Lucky is just a
     compressed, rebellious ball of glowing pink-and-green cosmic slime stuffed into an
     escape pod.
  ●​ The Gag (Dialogue): A massive, harsh intercom speaker is barking at Lucky. It’s Lucky’s
     Dad, a high-ranking Bad Guy executive. The speech balloons take up a lot of space, filled
     with exhausting, out-of-touch parental complaints mixed with fascist corporate-speak:
        ○​ Dad (Intercom): "I just don't understand the whole nonbinary thing. Why can't you
            pick a tangible demographic to market yourself to? If you'd just join The Vanguard,
            you could be financially successful and oppress people with a dental plan!"
PAGE 2 – THE DROP AND THE CONTRAIL
  ●​ Toon Level: 3
  ●​ 4-5 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1: Close up on Lucky inside the pod. A pseudopod of slime shoots up and

     mid-sentence with a wet SQUELCH.
  ●​ Panel 2: Lucky hits a giant, unnecessarily red "EJECT" button.
  ●​ Panel 3: The pod fires out of the spaceship and immediately breaks apart upon hitting

     Looming "WORK IS LOVE" billboards are dwarfed by the screaming, neon
     pink-and-green slime contrail Lucky leaves across the sky, arcing directly downward.

PAGE 6 – THE SIGNAL REACHES NORTH

  ●​ 4 Panels (Montage).

  ●​ Panel 2: The wave travels fast over highways and suburbs, heading north.

PAGE 7 – CITY SOUND REHEARSAL & BAJONKA
  ●​ Toon Level: 0 to 1
  ●​ 5 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1 (Wide): Exterior/hallway shot of "City Sound Rehearsal Studio" in Minneapolis.

     flyers.
  ●​ Panel 2: Ryan arrives. He’s a thoroughly normal, stoic guy in a heavy flannel shirt and

     hardware and cymbals in his arms, balancing it all with Northern Minnesotan efficiency.
  ●​ Panel 3: He approaches the heavy soundproof door for Room 4. Sitting perfectly still

  ●​ Panel 5: A classic comic time-skip gag. We don't see how it happens, but Ryan is walking
     through the now-open heavy door. Bajonka is sitting in the exact same pose, but the
     clipboard is gone.

  ●​ 4-5 Panels.

  ●​ Toon Level: 3 (High Physics Break)

     sideways, tearing the gutter.

  ●​ The Splash: Ryan completely transcends his human form. His body dissolves entirely
     into pure, swirling cosmic matter, igniting into a terrifying, beautiful purplish-black glow. He
