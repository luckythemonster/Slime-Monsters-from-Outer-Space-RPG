PAGE 1 – THE ORBITAL MANSION (Beat 1)
  ●​ Toon Level: 1
  ●​ 4 Panels.
  ●​ Panel 1 (Wide): Establish the location. A massive, pristine orbital space-mansion floating

     minimalist tech gadget. White curves, seamless glass, and dozens of sleek, luxury
     space-yachts docked outside.
  ●​ Panel 2: Put-putting toward this beacon of extreme wealth is the band's completely
     rusted, slime-painted Earth space-van.
  ●​ Panel 3: Inside the van. Phoenix is piloting, gripping the steering wheel so tightly their
     gray hands are shaking. Sweat is actively dripping down their glasses.
  ●​ Panel 4: Close up on Phoenix, looking terrified out the windshield at the multi-billion-credit
     yachts.
        ○​ Phoenix: "My brain thinks if we so much as scratch the paint on one of these
            yachts, I will be legally required to sell my vital organs on the Vanguard black
            market."

  ●​ 4 Panels.
