# Slime Monsters from Outer Space — JRPG Story Bible (Set-Piece Lens)

A SNES-style JRPG (early FF / Chrono Trigger / Earthbound) in twelve chapters on two "discs." Every chapter owns one unforgettable map, one gimmick boss, and comedy the player *does* rather than reads. Battles are gigs: the shared party meter is the **KRAAANG meter**; filling it raises the fight's **Toon Level (0–4)**, and at Toon 4 the art ruptures; sound-effect words are physical objects here, so a full meter summons one.

## Chapter outline

### Chapter 1 — Coagulation (VERTICAL SLICE)

**Locations:** Star-cruiser drop-pod bay (Planet California orbit) · Freefall over Minneapolis · Alley behind a dive bar · Phoenix's apartment · Ryan's garage (Northern Minnesota) · City Sound Rehearsal, Room 4 · Palmer's Bar.
**Party:** Lucky → +Phoenix → +Brian → +Ryan; Bajonka joins as field manager. Ends: Lucky, Phoenix, Bajonka.

**Beats:**
1. **The intercom (Toon 1).** Cold open in the pod. Dad's balloon fills the screen: *"I just don't understand the whole nonbinary thing. Why can't you pick a tangible demographic to market yourself to? If you'd just join The Vanguard, you could be financially successful and oppress people with a dental plan!"* Tutorial verb: hold-and-release a pseudopod that **slaps the balloon mid-sentence** (SQUELCH) — the Ch9 boss mechanic. Hit the unnecessarily red EJECT.
2. **Freefall minigame (Toon 3, `Freefall` scene).** Steer the slime-squirrel contrail through "WORK IS LOVE" billboards; each glitch scores *Obnoxiousness*; max speed forms spiky slime-sunglasses. Target: the alley.
3. **The pothole (Toon 2).** KRATHOOM. Dumpsters fall. Phoenix doesn't flinch. *"My brain thinks you're going to have to pay the city for that pothole."* Key item: **POTHOLE INVOICE — $1,400.** Never payable, never leaves the inventory.
4. **Coagulation (Toon 1).** Pop-pop-pop. Rant tree: three complaints, each matched by a Phoenix grievance (college, collapse, rent). *"I want to make the loudest, most obnoxious noise possible until this whole empire gets a migraine."* / *"Beats the day job."* Phoenix joins.
5. **The cosmic Craigslist ad (Toon 2).** *"We need a drummer. I could put a flyer up at the coffee shop..."* / *"Too slow. I'm going to ask the universe."* Hold to pour slime into the amp jack; at full charge the UI border bends outward. Montage: "PRODUCTIVITY IS FREEDOM" glitches, the wave rolls north, **Ryan freezes in his garage, sticks hovering.** Phoenix puts the flyer up anyway. "Just in case."
6. **Brian (Toon 0).** The flyer delivers first: Zappa shirt, pedalboard the size of a door. "Your ad said drummer. I play guitar. But I have a pedalboard." Phoenix: "We have four dollars." Brian joins (2nd). Commands: **Normal Chord** (1 damage, no meter) and **Turn the Bass Down** (debuffs Phoenix: *"I'm not a 'man,' Brian. And no."*).
7. **Brian knows a drummer.** "Ryan. My cousin. He will never answer a text." Subaru north to the garage; Ryan still frozen from the wave. Lucky's cosmic pitches only make his caption colder ([Weird.] [Very weird.]). Brian: "Forty bucks and gas money." Ryan: *"Yep."* Brian got him in the room.
8. **City Sound, Room 4 (Toon 0–1).** Ryan arrives with a physically impossible amount of hardware. Bajonka outside the door with a clipboard. Nod; sneeze; smash cut — door open, clipboard gone. *"You made good time from the transmission. You cool with playing fast?"* / *"Yep."* / *"My brain thinks we should have asked his rate first. We have exactly four dollars and a half-ounce of weed."*
9. **TUTORIAL BATTLE — "Room 4" (Toon 3).** Enemy: THE SONG (a tempo bar). *"One, two, three, four!"* `onRound(1)`: the eruption splatters Ryan; `transform` to possessed sheet; his attack becomes a multi-hit octopus smear. Rigid captions: [Well. I guess my arms belong to the goo now.] [Tempo's good, though. Very steady.] [Ride cymbal sounds a bit tinny. Should probably buy a new one if I survive this.] *"Holy shit. You're a machine."* / [I am literally a hostage.] / *"So, do I get the gig?"* Bajonka drops a band agreement on the snare. Ryan and Bajonka join.
10. **ONE MONTH LATER (duct-taped card).** *"Do we really need all the political stickers? We're going to alienate the market."* [I give this guy three weeks. Tops.] Mini-battle: the first full meter summons **KRAAANG**; its G swings like a bat into Brian's iced coffee; the pedalboard sparks and dies. *"I can't work like this! I hate this weird, creative shit! Can't we just play normal music?!"*
11. **THE MISINTERPRETATION (playable comedy).** Lucky unplugs with a POP; slime bristles like a cat. Three calm choices — *"You need to stop being an asshole, or you are fired."* / "We clearly have some fundamental differences…" / "…don't bring it to rehearsal." Pick any. All three resolve to: *"Did you just say my foundation is broken?! Are you calling my house crooked?!"* *"My brain thinks you might be having a stroke."* / *"I literally didn't say anything about a house..."* Door slams. [Well. His timing was terrible anyway.] Brian leaves. Bajonka drops the flyer: **GIG TONIGHT: PALMER'S BAR – NO GUITAR SOLO NECESSARY.**
12. **Palmer's, pre-show (Toon 1).** NPC roam: Warner dry-heaving after a Rat Fight opener; a very tall guy who says the ceiling "has a heavy aura, man" (Diego, seeded); a glued-on mohawk. *"Dispatch, I have located the unauthorized slime gathering. Stand by."*
13. **BOSS GIG — PALMER'S BAR (Toon 2→4).** See Boss.
14. **Aftermath (Toon 1).** *"Okay. That is our sound."* / *"My brain thinks we are officially domestic terrorists."* / *"I think I broke the space-time continuum on that snare fill."* New beat: Ryan's hands are still vibrating. Out loud: "I'm done." Lucky's choice menu — three responses, all magenta, all wrong; the color won't come down. Ryan leaves the party.
15. **Stinger.** Bajonka trots out from under drywall with van keys and an itinerary: **POTATO BELT: TOMORROW** circled in red. The only two who didn't flee: Diego and Warner, holding his sticks: "I know a drummer." NEXT: THE POTATO BELT.

**Boss — PALMER'S BAR (Structural Integrity 100%).** The venue is the enemy and you *want* to kill it. Riffs damage Integrity; the undercover cop is a second target whose **Dispatch** bar ticks each round he survives (full = raid, retry). `onRound(3)`: [I've been fighting the possession. That was a mistake.] — and the only choice Ryan gets all game: **[Keep fighting it] / [Let go].** Fighting it ticks Dispatch; **Let go** triggers Toon 4: backdrop swapped for a painted cosmic-horror plate, UI melts, Ryan dissolves into **The Eternity**, his attacks become gravity-weighted SFX (DOOM, CRACK, THUD) striking the ceiling. [I am not trapped in the goo. The goo is trapped in the pocket.] [Rhythm achieved.] Integrity 0: roof peels like a sardine can, walls blow outward. The player chose "let go" — and Ryan pays for it. That is why the quitting stings.

**Stinger:** beat 15.

## Midpoint design

**Why Planets Collide is the hinge.** Disc 1 is about finding a sound and losing the people who make it (Brian, Ryan, Warner, the apartment). Disc 2 is about getting them back (Billy, Ryan, a guitar, a vehicle). The collision wipes the board — guitar, van, home, planet — so Disc 2 rebuilds every learned system slightly askew: Voice riffs before a guitar, synth before a bass, a pod before a barge.

**The unwinnable fight, honestly.** Autosave on the roof. The HUD tells the truth: real damage numbers, but Kurt's bar is labeled **GRAVITY**, not HP, and climbs each round regardless. Sticky takes self-damage every turn forcing 13/8 to wait; Diego is Reverent for three rounds (*"Man. Listen to that LOW END."*). Round 4: the yacht hyperspaces — the villain leaving is the tell. Round 6: the second planet fills the backdrop. The last prompt is one button: **ONE MORE CHORD.** Black, no music, no SFX. **INTERMISSION** in plain white type, then a fake card — *PLEASE TURN THE CARTRIDGE OVER* — held a beat too long. No Game Over screen; the debris field is the only continue. Chapter 11 reuses the identical HUD so the player feels the moment the gravity bar *stalls*.

**Re-pacing the front half.** Issues 5 and 6 merge into one Embargo chapter; Issue 3's dome shatter becomes a chapter cliffhanger; Disc 2 is seeded in Disc 1 (NO DENTAL sticker, Ch1; the Trunk Fund and Pete's brother, Ch4; Kurt's disqualification, Ch5).

## Billy Ruben

**Who.** A bilirubin-orange slime (sun-bleached; the joke in the name), bassist and wall-of-sound engineer, now pirate DJ of Radio Free California inside a hollowed-out "WORK IS LOVE" billboard; the barge is the amp.

**Backstory.** Lucky and Billy were NO DENTAL, two slimes on the basement circuit under the billboards. Their biggest show was raided on Dad's orders; Lucky was dragged onto the star-cruiser — the pod bay on screen one is where that night ends — and Billy was left on the floor. Lucky assumed Billy was arrested, or gave up, or sold out, and hasn't said the name since. Billy kept the amp. When Lucky "asked the universe" in Chapter 1, the wave reached Planet California; Billy has followed its echoes across the galaxy, one gig behind, ever since.

**The Han Solo moment (Ch9, beat 8).** Dad's star-cruiser — the ship from the first screen — descends, tractor beam on Lucky; the power is cut, the amps are dead, the party's commands grey out one by one. *"My brain thinks this is the part where we get re-podded."* Then every billboard on the planet plays the NO DENTAL riff at once, a converted billboard-barge drops out of hyperspace, refrigerators glowing, and rakes the beam emitter with a wall of sound. The beam stutters; the hubcap guitar draws power from the broadcast; the player takes the shot. Billy, over the radio: "You left the amp on, dude. I could hear you from here."

**Why here.** It is the literal Death Star: the ship Lucky escaped from, come back for them. The chapter asks whether Lucky's old life was real or just something they fled, and Billy is the answer — a bandmate who stayed and never held the leaving against them. That forgiveness is the hinge for Chapter 10: Billy forgives Lucky for leaving so Lucky can forgive Ryan for leaving. It also makes the barge the Disc 2 vehicle. At the final boss it would steal the band's win; here, the finale stays theirs.

## Running gags, motifs, themes

- **Color is the HUD.** Lucky's slime is the mood meter the player reads before the dialogue.
- **Three voices, three text boxes:** "My brain thinks…" (wobbly), Ryan's rigid squares, Diego's lowercase auras.
- **Bajonka's escalation:** clipboard → contract → flyer → van keys → guard → Denim Boys → claymore → dossier → escape pod → prison break → the yacht's title paperwork.
- **The Pothole Invoice** rides in the inventory to the credits. "My brain thinks they'll find us."
- **Countdowns that are never money:** eviction, coupon timer, Day 14/30, 00:00:01, the fuel gauge.
- **Clipboards on both sides.** The Eclipse and the Vanguard are one bureaucracy with different stickers.
- **SFX are objects.** KRAAANG knocks over coffee; DOOM breaks a roof; Terms & Conditions are bricks; tweets are birds.
- **Cops in bad disguises**, escalating: mohawk, khakis, Intern Unit 74-B, a mop.
- **13/8:** undanceable, unmarketable, the only thing that shatters paywalls.
- **Letting go vs. holding on.** Ryan lets go and runs; Lucky holds on and rages; Phoenix lets go of "my brain thinks" and becomes something scarier. The count-off is how you forgive.
- **Theme:** creative labor under a fascist corporate state. The empire gets a migraine; you're still broke.
