# Side quest: MIC CHECK

Status: the cast, David, the optional status and the outcome are *(canon, Lucky)*. Stops, mechanics and every line of dialogue are proposal.

## One line

Lucky's ultimate weapon was the voice, and they had it the whole time. Three frontpeople and one cave get them to notice.

## What it is

- **Optional.** Lucky is the singer at the end of the game either way. The quest unlocks the ultimate skill, **MIGRAINE**, and the scene where Lucky understands why.
- **When.** Chapter 11, from the hub on Kurt's shoulder, after the morning Lucky wakes up clear and Diego leaves. It can be done before or after the prison.
- **State.** Lucky does all of it clear. Nobody in this quest cures anything. Lucky leaves with technique, not a feeling.
- **Why it is true.** Chapter 7 already made the voice a weapon: with the guitar gone, Lucky screamed the Vanguard's fine print and it hit like a brick. The comic's ending is won by Lucky putting the instrument down and talking. The quest only points at what is already there.

## The frontpeople

All three are shirtless or close to it. None of them is impressed by the hubcap guitar.

**JIM.** A skinny, muscular, shirtless iguana-man with blonde hair. He is only ever called Jim, including by Jim.
- *Where:* the debris field from Chapter 7, on the one rock that gets sun.
- *The thing about him:* he is cold-blooded. He is sluggish and nearly asleep until a light hits him, and then nothing in the sector can stop him.
- *Lesson: BODY.* The voice starts below the neck. Jim: "Jim's cold. Jim's always cold. Jim gets under the light anyway." It is the one thing anybody says to Lucky about being clear, and he is talking about himself.
- *Unlocks:* **STAGE DIVE.** Lucky can spend CROWD instead of Breath.

**HANK.** A super-buff, shirtless beast-man with a giant brain.
- *Where:* the Earth-Remnant Library from Chapter 3. He trains there because it is the hardest room in the universe to be loud in. He bench-presses the Reference section. The Terrifying Librarian shushes everyone but him.
- *The thing about him:* he is a brain and a body at once and sees no gap between them. Phoenix: "My brain thinks he could kill us." Hank: "Whose brain?" / "...Mine." / "Then say that." It is the first push on Phoenix's last line.
- *Lesson: BREATH.* Endurance. One whole clause on one breath, under the Shush threshold, every day, whether or not you feel like it. His only note: "Again."
- *Unlocks:* Breath maximum goes up, and long clauses no longer drop.

**The fractal-man.** Big poofy hair. His name is longer than the universe.
- *Where:* the empty main stage at South by South-Wasteland, from Chapter 4.
- *The thing about him:* the game's rule is that sounds are objects, so his name is one. When he introduces himself the nameplate starts printing and does not stop. It leaves the text box, crosses the stage and runs off the edge of the map. Lucky walks across it to reach him. Phoenix: "My brain thinks we don't have that kind of time."
- *Lesson: RANGE.* The voice is an instrument. He says every sentence at three sizes: "Sing the line. Now sing the line inside the line."
- *Unlocks:* vocal lines split and land on the off-grid beats, which is where STICKY-R lives.

## The cave

After all three, a hatch opens in Kurt's chest. The band has been riding him for three chapters and nobody knew he was hollow. Rain collected in the rust during the dirge in Chapter 8, and there is a swamp in there now.

**Ignis and his band.** *(canon, Lucky)* Ignis Fatuus, a swamp singer who moved in during the grief. His nameplate reads IGNIS. An ignis fatuus is a will-o'-the-wisp, the false light over a swamp that leads travelers in, and he is the light at the hatch. He did not come alone.

- **Pierre Edison II.** A horrifying sludge monster that looks like two men fused together. He plays two guitars at once.
- **A nameless ghost** on bass. His nameplate is blank. Nobody asks.
- **Kyle.** A tentacled swamp monster who can play ultra-fast sludge beats.

**Loud first.** *(canon, Lucky)* When Lucky comes through the hatch the four of them are mid-song at full sludge, the heaviest thing in the game since Kurt's chord, and Ignis is hitting the notes hard. He has all the power Lucky has ever wanted. Nobody in this cave is quiet because they have to be.

**Then the band falls away.** *(canon, Lucky)* The ghost can only hold a shape for the length of a song. His bass line thins and he is gone. Pierre Edison II stops, one guitar and then the other. Kyle lays his sticks across the snare. They will not play without him. He is back for the next song, every time. Ignis keeps singing. Alone it comes out as quiet swamp goth blues: the game's music ducks to nothing and his text prints small. It is the same voice. It is what is left when the band is gone, and Lucky, whose band has been falling away since Chapter 1, is standing there clear with a guitar they have not played in days.

*(All four are original characters and nothing they say is borrowed.)*

He looks at the hubcap guitar: "You won't need that."

**[Bring it] / [Leave it]**

- **[Bring it].** At the back of the cave stands a figure at full magenta with a guitar: every color Lucky lost, as loud as the game gets. Every hit Lucky lands makes both of them louder. The fight can be won. When the figure drops, the face is Lucky's, in beige: the human half, Dad's side. *(canon, Lucky)* Results screen: YOU WON. YOU GOT: NOTHING. Ignis, outside: "Again?" The cave can be re-entered.
- **[Leave it].** The same figure, the same volume. Lucky has no attack. At the bottom of the menu is one entry that has never been there: a plain line of talk, at normal size. It takes two presses. *(canon, Lucky)*
  - Lucky: "You can turn down." It is what Brian, Dad and the Librarian have all said to Lucky. The figure gets louder.
  - Lucky: "I can hear you." It stops.
  - In the silence the figure says one word, in small text: "...Check." It is a mic check. It has been as loud as the game gets for twelve chapters, and what it wanted to know was whether anything was coming through.
  - Lucky: "I've got you." It sits down. It is Lucky.
  - Results screen: LUCKY LEARNED MIGRAINE.

This is the game's fourth honest fight: Palmer's cannot be lost, the Sludge Duel cannot be won, Bajonka cannot be hit, and the cave can be won the wrong way.

**MIGRAINE.** The Chapter 1 line, made into a command: the loudest, most obnoxious noise possible, until the whole empire gets one. It spends all of Lucky's Breath. It is learned by being quiet once. Its menu text mirrors the cave: "You can turn up. They'll hear you." *(new)* Before the cave Lucky is loud because nobody is listening. After it, loud is a choice.

## David

*(canon, Lucky)* David is human. He is the sound guy from the VFW. He plays guitar at the end.

- **Where he comes from (proposal).** Diego's road trip. The last postcard is from a VFW hall, Veterans of Foreign Worlds: "The sound guy here is the best guitarist I've ever heard, man. He's never played a show. He just turns other people up." Diego moved to synth, so the guitar chair was his to fill. At the airlock, "I brought one" means a vibe and a guitarist.
- **Slot.** A guest for the last chapter, the way Alibi is in Chapter 10. *(canon, Lucky)*
- **Voice.** He talks like a sound guy: check, levels, less of everything. He has spent years making other bands sound good.
- **The handoff.** Lucky has not touched the hubcap guitar since the fence. At the load-in they hold it out. David: "What's it tuned to?" Lucky: "Loud." He looks at the nuclear amplifier: "Is this up to code?" Nobody answers. Then Lucky, at the mic, asks the oldest singer question there is: "Can I get more me?" David: "You've got all of you." *(new)*
- **Why him.** A singer needs a sound guy. David is the first person in the game who turns Lucky up.

## What it changes in the finale

- Lucky sings from the load-in on. The hubcap guitar and the nuclear amplifier are David's.
- Phase 3: Lucky stops singing. The band falls out, David last. Lucky says the fourth-wall line in a talking voice. If the cave was done right, this is the second quiet thing they have ever said.
- The comic's last beat survives: Lucky lights the joint off the plasma strings of the guitar David is holding.
