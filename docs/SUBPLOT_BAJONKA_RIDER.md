# Subplot: The Rider (Bajonka and Lucky)

Status: proposal. Everything here is *(new)* until Lucky says otherwise. Written against `docs/comic-outlines/` at commit 6966d9b and the patched `STORY_BIBLE.md` (Bajonka's Chapter 9 rescue; Lucky counts the band in).

## One line

The one who never left asks for one thing. She asks the only way she can: in writing.

## What it is about

Disc 2 is "who comes back." Lucky spends the game chasing a drummer who ran. Bajonka is the only name in the character table that reads "Ch 1 to end." She has been at every save point. The subplot makes Lucky look at her.

## Canon it hangs on

- **She never speaks as a dog.** So the proposal is paperwork. A contract rider is the gig word for a wish list, and venues always get the rider slightly wrong.
- **The band agreement grows a page per save** (`contractPages = 3 + saveCount` in the slice). The player has been initialing it all game without reading it.
- **The clipboard arc.** Slapped away (Ch 2), signed blind (Ch 3), read (Ch 7). Reading is the unlock.
- **Asking the universe.** Lucky asked for a drummer and got Brian. Warner showed up three weeks late. The universe delivers, late and adjacent.
- **"Next: Princess Bajonka vs. Pete."** The Issue 4 caption. The word "Princess" is already hers.
- **Color is the HUD.** Beige is Dad.
- **She broke the beam.** *(canon, Lucky)* In Chapter 9 the one who comes for Lucky on Dad's roof is Bajonka, in a stolen billboard. Nobody from Lucky's old life shows. The rider is the first thing she has ever asked for in return.
- **The unwinnable pair.** Palmer's cannot be lost. The Sludge Duel cannot be won. This is the third one.

## New pieces it needs

1. **A Bajonka summon.** Not in DESIGN.md yet. Proposed: command **BAJONKA**, unlocked at the Potato Belt dock ("Hey... is that a dog?"). The camera cuts to an exterior wall. THUD. One non-boss enemy is gone, stacked like cordwood. On a boss she drops a contract and it loses a turn reading. Cost: one page added to the band agreement. The more you lean on her, the deeper the rider is buried. In Chapter 9 the command greys out with every other one under the tractor beam, and she comes anyway.
2. **Lucky is half human, and the human half is Dad.** *(canon, Lucky)* Mom is a rainbow slime monster who lives in an alternate dimension, Anozira. Dad is a human corporate fascist. This makes the HUD heredity: every color Lucky turns is Mom's side, and beige is Dad's. A human who married a rainbow has spent Lucky's whole life asking them to pick one color. The band has never heard any of this before the scene.
3. **The rider.** The last page of the band agreement, there since Room 4.

## Beats

1. **Seeds, Disc 1.** The agreement Bajonka drops on the snare has a last page headed RIDER; inspecting it: "Lucky does not read it." Her Ch 4 playable sequence opens on the title card PRINCESS BAJONKA VS. PETE. In Ch 5 Lucky builds the Sanctuary out of her fur and calls it biomass.
2. **READ (Ch 7 on).** After Lucky reads the Promoter's clipboard, Bajonka's save prompt gains a fourth option: READ. The rider has one item: management requests the hand of the party of the first part in marriage.
3. **The first no.** Lucky: "I can't marry a dog. I'm half human." A beige edge appears on Lucky's HUD, the first time outside Dad's office. Phoenix: "My brain thinks that is new information." Diego: "Which half, man?" Lucky, after a beat too long: "...Dad's." The beige edge holds. Phoenix: "My brain thinks your dad married a rainbow." Lucky has no answer. The objection is hypocrisy on arrival: Lucky exists because a human married across the line Lucky is now drawing, and they are drawing it in his voice.
4. **The amendment.** Bajonka takes the clipboard back. She crosses nothing out. She adds a line: ONE (1) NIGHT AS A LADY. She sets the rider on the amp and sneezes into the input jack. The wave rolls out the way it did in Chapter 1.
5. **Delivery (Chapter 10, the night after the fence, before the prison gig).** *(canon, Lucky)* Camp on Kurt's shoulder. Title card: PRINCESS BAJONKA. A full magical transformation at Toon 4, then a hard drop to Toon 0. On the couch sits an elderly Thai woman with reading glasses and the clipboard. The universe converted her age out of dog years. She is Thai because Bajonka was born in Phuket, Thailand. *(canon, Lucky)* The nameplate reads KHUN BAJONKA. She sneezes. The text box opens and stays empty for as long as every silence she has ever held. Then she speaks. *(canon, Lucky: she speaks as a human; the lines below are proposal)* Her first words: "I have notes."
6. **Notes.** One per band member, the way a manager gives them after a set. Phoenix: "My brain had questions when we met her. My brain chose not to ask them. My brain would like to ask them now." Her note for Phoenix: "Your brain is right more often than you let it be." Diego finally asks how she tied those knots without thumbs: "Practice." Sticky: "Four-four is not coming. Stop waiting for it." Kurt, who is the venue: "Hold still." Lucky gets no note. Lucky gets the clipboard. Everyone treats her exactly as they always have: as the most competent person present.
7. **The license.** She holds out the clipboard. It is a marriage license with her half filled in. NAME: Bajonka. OCCUPATION: Management. PLACE OF BIRTH: Phuket, Thailand. Lucky's half is blank. Lucky: "You're from Phuket?" Khun Bajonka: "Nobody asked." Phoenix: "My brain thinks she was at my back door before you landed, and neither of us asked her one question." Diego: "Phuket's closer than Anozira, man." The universe did not get this rider wrong. It is the first order it has ever filled correctly. The band had the wrong picture, because the band never asked.
8. **The choice.** INITIAL HERE? **[I do] / [I can't]**

**Her voice.** Short and itemized, the way she has always worked. She states; she does not ask. She will say what and when and never how, so speaking explains nothing the silence was protecting. She speaks the game's language plainly; nothing about the way she talks is the joke. She sneezes in both forms.

**The engine of the joke.** Lucky's stated objection is now void. She is entirely human. Lucky is only half. It was Dad's objection to begin with, borrowed. Whatever reluctance is left belongs to Lucky, and it is the same thing it has been all game: Lucky commits to people who leave. The reluctance never points at her age or where she is from. If it does, the scene sours and the joke moves to the wrong target.

## Branch A: [I do] "One Night Only"

The night is a gig: load in, set, load out. The set is her ceremony. *(canon, Lucky: the ceremony is hers, a Thai Buddhist wedding in the Central pattern, which is also how most Buddhist weddings in the South are done. Every beat below is proposal.)*

**What she brought.** A conch shell, a sealed bottle of water, a loop of white cotton thread and a stack of envelopes. Nobody has seen any of it before. The water was blessed in Phuket. She has had it the whole time. There are no monks on Kurt's shoulder, and she does not stage the morning rite without them.

**Her side.** *(canon, Lucky: the Soy Boys are at the wedding; staging is proposal)* When the procession turns toward the couch, there is a squad of four standing at attention behind it. The Soy Boys: elite vegan animal rights special operatives. The leader is a square-jawed commando with a white high-and-tight. Beside him are a huge brawler, a stealth operative, and one with an Austrian accent. (Call signs, proposal: EXTRA FIRM, SEITAN, SILKEN, TEMPEH.) Nobody saw them arrive, and nobody notices the stealth one until he has been in the scene for a while. Phoenix: "My brain thinks we don't know those guys." They do not speak, they do not sit, and they do not take their eyes off her. Why they are here is not explained.

**Load in: the procession.** Lucky's side walks to her house, which is the couch, carrying trays. The trays hold everything the band owns: one potato and a debt. Sticky plays the long drum, in 13/8. Diego: "I'm ordained, man." Khun Bajonka: "Not needed." She gives him the job the custom has for him instead: he is Lucky's senior, and he will speak for them.

**The gates.** A silver gate and then a gold one stand between the procession and the bride, and her friends and female relatives hold them. She has none here. The Soy Boys do not presume. Phoenix looks at the empty gate for a while, walks over and picks up one end. Sticky takes the other. Each gate costs an envelope, and the gold one costs more.
- Silver: Lucky's envelope holds an IOU. Sticky also wants a task: thirteen push-ups. Slime push-ups.
- Gold: Phoenix names the price. "My brain thinks the toll is you stop being mad at me." It has been sitting there since the clipping in Chapter 9. Lucky: "...Fine." The gate opens.

**The proposal and the dowry.** Diego makes the proposal on Lucky's behalf: "They're a good hang, man. They show up." The dowry goes on the table to be checked. It is the Pothole Invoice, $1,400 of debt. Making a show of how heavy it is belongs to the bride's mother. There is no mother, so she does it herself: one sheet of paper, lifted like an anvil. Then she hands it back, the way it is usually done now. It lists two debtors.

**The wai.** Both of them, to each other. Lucky presses two slime hands together. SQUELCH.

**Respect to the elders.** The couple honors the oldest people present, who answer with a blessing. She is the oldest person present by decades. The only other elder is the venue. They bow to Kurt. He answers with one note, very low.

**The water.** They kneel side by side with their hands out, joined by the cotton thread: one piece, two loops, one on each head. Lucky's sinks in a little. Each guest pours from the conch over their hands, says a wish, leaves an envelope, and gets a keepsake.
- The pour is the Chapter 8 slow-beat input, its only use outside a fight. Spill it and she adds a line to the agreement: MISSED CUE, $5.
- Diego: "May your vibes be joint, man."
- Phoenix: "My brain thinks... I hope it works." The humidity gives them MELTING, as it did in the Potato Belt.
- Sticky pours exactly thirteen drops.
- The Soy Boys pour in formation, one after another, without spilling a drop. Each leaves a sealed envelope. Nobody learns what is in them. Instead of a wish, each one salutes. The big one is crying and does not break formation.
- Kurt cannot hold a conch. One drop off his fingertip soaks the couch.
- Water makes slime run. Lucky's hands start to come apart. She closes hers around them: "Hold still."
- The envelopes hold whatever each of them had. The keepsake is an itemized receipt.

**The reception.** Sticky's cake: challah, braided, thirteen tiers. The one with the Austrian accent reads the ingredients off Sticky's chest plate: "It has eggs." The Soy Boys stand at attention beside a cake they cannot eat. Phoenix reads the license: "My brain thinks community property means she owns half the pothole." Lucky finally asks about Chapter 6: "Why the cymbal? Why not my guitar?" Khun Bajonka: "You could build another guitar." It is the one real answer she gives all night, and only this branch hears it.

**The bed.** Making it up falls to an older couple with a long marriage behind them. Nobody qualifies. Phoenix: "My brain and I have been together my whole life." They make the bed. It is the escape pod from Chapter 6, with cans tied to it. The rest of the rite, lying down and waking to tell each other a lucky dream, is the part she does not get.

- **Load out: curfew.** *(canon, Lucky: she turns back before the wedding night; nothing happens)* Every venue has a hard curfew and so does the universe. The rider said one night, and it ends at load out. Lucky carries her to the hatch, palest pink on the HUD, visibly out of excuses. At the threshold she calls it herself, the way a manager does: "Curfew." It is her last word. Lucky is holding a dog. She sneezes, hops down, circles twice and goes to sleep on the snare case. Lucky takes the couch. The toast, in the slice's own voice: "Lucky married Bajonka. That is all that happened."
- **Morning.** A ring on her collar. Lucky: "...I'm married to a dog." Phoenix: "My brain thinks you were always going to be." Lucky says it clear. *(canon, Lucky)* The morning after, in both branches, Lucky has no color at all (bible, Chapter 11). Marrying her does not prevent it and it is not about her.

**The marriage holds.** *(canon, Lucky)* Technically, legally, on paper, which is the only place Bajonka has ever done anything. It is never consummated and the game says so once, plainly, in the toast, so no player has to wonder.

Rewards: key item **MARRIAGE LICENSE**, filed next to the Pothole Invoice, which now lists two debtors. Lucky is on her dental plan. It is the first dental Lucky has ever been offered that did not come from Dad.

**The new summon power.** *(canon, Lucky, that there is one; name and effect are proposal)* **SPOUSAL PRIVILEGE.** The BAJONKA command stays as it was and this sits under it, once per chapter. The cut-in is the only place the lady ever appears again: Khun Bajonka, one turn, reading glasses on, serving the enemy papers. One word: "Served." A boss loses its next action and its strongest buff is struck through in red. No page cost. The one night is over; the paperwork is forever.

## Branch B: [I can't]

Lucky reaches for a second reason and does not have one.

**Boss: KHUN BAJONKA, "Irreconcilable Differences" (Toon 0, unwinnable).** No music, static camera: her register from the Pete sequences. She has no HP bar and cannot be targeted. Lucky has an HP bar and it never moves. She does not attack.

**Lucky cannot attack her at all.** *(canon, Lucky)* RIFF, STRUM and every offensive command are grey. The cursor still lands on them and the help line reads: "Lucky won't." It is the game's third grey-out, after the embargo and the tractor beam, and the first one that is Lucky's own doing.

That leaves one command: **EXCUSE**, a list. "I'm half human" is at the top, already struck through. Each excuse costs a turn. She answers each with one line and takes back one thing she handled: *(lines are proposal)*

- "We're on tour." / "I booked it." The itinerary. The turn order goes blank.
- "You're my manager." / "I know what I am." The band agreement. The party sits down. Phoenix: "My brain thinks I'm staying out of this."
- "It's not you." / "It never is." The van keys. RUN is gone.
- "I'm not ready." / "You have never been ready. I handled it." The flyer.

Each used excuse is struck through in red. When the list is empty the only entry left is "...". She takes the clipboard: "That's everything." The BAJONKA command is struck through. The battle ends. Nobody was hit. The results screen reads: BAJONKA RECOVERED: EVERYTHING SHE BROUGHT.

Palmer's could not be lost. The Sludge Duel could not be won. Here the numbers are honest again: Lucky finishes at full HP and has lost anyway.

**After.** At curfew she is a dog, in the save pose. In the morning Lucky is clear, the same as in the other branch; refusing her did not cause it. She saves the game for the rest of the story. She does not speak again and she does not sneeze again. The summon slot shows the same blank dash Lucky's instrument slot showed at the end of Disc 1. The contract stops growing. She still runs the prison break alone and flawlessly. She never fails. That was never the question.

## Third state: never read

READ is optional. If the player never uses it, nothing happens, the summon stays, and the rider rides unread to the credits. Signed blind, forever.

## Open questions for Lucky

1. **A trace of Mom.** Settled: she is a rainbow slime monster, she lives in Anozira, she does not appear, and she is not coming. Anozira is not "the universe" and she grants nothing. *Default:* no trace at all beyond Lucky's colors and Diego's one line. *Alternative:* a color Dad's office is missing.
2. **Backing out mid-fight.** *Default:* no. Like Ryan's choice, it is made once. *Alternative:* when the EXCUSE list is empty, the last entry is not "..." but "I do."
3. **The cymbal answer.** *Default:* she says "You could build another guitar," in the [I do] branch only. *Alternative:* Lucky asks and she lets the question go, and Chapter 6 stays unexplained forever. *What changes:* whether obliging her earns the one thing the game has been withholding.
4. **The lady in the summon.** *Default:* Khun Bajonka appears in the SPOUSAL PRIVILEGE cut-in, one turn at a time, for the rest of the game. *Alternative:* the dog only; the lady was one night and is never seen again. *What changes:* whether the wish was a visit or a door left open.

## Before this ships

The ceremony was written from published descriptions, not from experience. It needs a read from someone who grew up with Thai weddings, ideally from the South, before it goes in the game. The comedy is meant to land on the band: a broke procession, a slime in water, nobody old enough to be an elder. If any beat reads as a joke about the custom itself, cut that beat.
