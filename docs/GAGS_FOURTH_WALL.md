# Gags: Lucky and the fourth wall

Status: Chapter 1's three lines (built), the 312 (DAMAGE) thread and David in the sound booth are *(canon, Lucky)*. Everything else is proposal, every line and mechanic *(new)*. Canon lines it leans on are quoted as they stand in `docs/STORY_BIBLE.md`.

## One line

Lucky spends the whole game complaining to the venue. The venue is the game. In Chapter 12 they finally look out at the room.

## Rules

1. **Lucky talks to the machinery, never to the player.** Menus, help lines, time cards, the win sting, the game-over screen, the save prompt, health bars. Until Chapter 12 Phase 3 Lucky never looks at the camera and never says "you" to the screen. The canon line ("An orange elephant? Really?") is the first time Lucky looks out, and every gag before it is what makes that look land. Disc 1 Lucky notices the machinery, Disc 2 Lucky argues with it, Phase 3 Lucky reviews it.
2. **The machinery answers in system text or not at all, and it never gives in.** Once, on the last card, it does.
3. **Nobody else does it.** The band treats Lucky's asides like weather, the way nobody mentions the jar. The one exception is Phoenix in Chapter 11 (below).
4. **The gags are a vital sign.** They stop the morning Lucky goes clear and come back in Phase 3 with the color. A player who notices they are gone has noticed Lucky is gone.
5. **Meta never explains anything the game holds back.** No gag touches the jar, why Bajonka saves the game, the Soy Boys' salute, what the poopoopeepee man's bands saw, or why one brother is named Earl.
6. **Never here:** Ryan's quit, UNIT 3, LUCKY: COME HOME, the fence, the rider's choice and Branch B, the cave, INTERMISSION, Dad's static, Laserstein's cell, anything in Chapter 11 about quitting.

## Running gags

Five threads. Each has a setup, a turn and a payoff, so the player learns the joke before it pays.

| Thread | Setup | Turn | Payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The cards are wrong** | Ch 1 to 2: Lucky corrects time cards | Ch 5: a card is right, and that is worse | Ch 11: Phoenix tries. Ch 12: Lucky edits the last card and it stands |
| **IV, V, I** | Ch 2: Lucky hates the win sting | Ch 3: 13/8 once. Ch 5: Lucky slaps it. Ch 10: 13/8 for good | Ch 12: no sting; Lucky counts in the next gig |
| **Onboarding** | Ch 1: the tutorial box is beige | Ch 9: Dad wrote the tutorials | Ch 12: David writes them now |
| **The band broke up** | Ch 2: Lucky denies the game-over screen | Lucky has a new excuse every time | Ch 11: Phoenix denies it for them |
| **It's a number** *(canon, Lucky)* | Ch 2: Lucky pockets a damage number | Ch 3: tries it on rent | Ch 12: tries it on fuel |

## By chapter

### Chapter 1 *(canon, Lucky; built)*

- **The tutorial box.** After Dad's opening lines in the pod bay, the game's first tutorial opens in the onboarding window: beige rule, beige text, no name tag. "TIP: Face the speaker and press CONFIRM to engage." Lucky, to the box: "Who's that?" Dad, on the intercom: "That's onboarding." Then the player engages with the speaker, by slapping it.
- **Every how-to line after it is beige too,** and nobody mentions it: the guitar case's "EQUIP is in the menu, for when there is something else.", the bass stand's "EQUIP it on Phoenix from the menu.", the drone's three tutorial toasts, "Hit the chorus." before the rehearsal and in it, and the freefall's X: SKIP. Characters' lines stay white even when they teach (Phoenix's "Your color's doing a thing.").
- **TWENTY MINUTES OF LIGHT RAIL LATER.** On the first ride only, off the train in the City Sound hallway, Lucky: "Twenty-six. There was a goose."
- **ONE WEEK LATER.** Lucky: "Eight days." Phoenix answers as if nobody spoke: "Nobody came." The card holds.

The later cards stay uncorrected: ONE MONTH LATER, THAT SUMMER, ALMOST A YEAR LATER and THE NIGHT BEFORE THE POTATO BELT TOUR. Zerkel Pit, the year with Brian and Ryan, and Palmer's stay clean.

### Chapter 2: Gig in the Potato Belt

- **THE NEXT MORNING.** Lucky: "It's two in the afternoon." Phoenix: "My brain thinks that's morning for a band."
- **Diego's portrait.** The first time the party menu shows four, Diego's head is cropped by the frame. Lucky: "Can somebody fix his picture?" Diego: "It's okay, man. I'm used to doorways." It is never fixed.
- **The win sting.** First fight with four. The sting plays (D, E, A: four, five, one, in 4/4). Lucky: "Four, five, one. Every time we win." Diego: "It's a happy progression, man." Lucky: "In four-four."
- **The Toll Booth.** The brief says Lucky says not to clip it, so clip it. If the player does: Lucky: "I said don't clip it." Phoenix: "My brain thinks I didn't." Lucky: "Then who did?" Nobody answers. Lucky does not look at the camera. This is rule 1's closest call in Disc 1, on purpose.
- **Parking.** After the third failed attempt the SKIP prompt appears. Lucky: "Parking gets a skip button?"
- **Nothing respawns.** The first time the band walks back through a map it cleared (the trench, if the player goes back to the van before the set), every fight is still gone. Lucky: "Nothing comes back here." Diego: "That's beautiful, man." Lucky: "It's not." It comes after "We don't say his name," and it is the one gag that is quietly about him. Nobody says so.
- **It's a number.** *(canon, Lucky: the thread; the lines are proposal)* Toon 2 makes SFX into objects, and damage numbers count. After the Riot Solo, Lucky's best GUITAR SWING crit is lying on the pallet stage and Lucky pockets it. At the dock, after "There is no door money": Lucky holds it out. "We did 312." The Promoter: "That's damage." Lucky: "It's a number." He moves on to section 4A. Key item: **312 (DAMAGE)**, named for the player's real best crit, so 312 here stands for whatever the player's number was. Description: "It's a number."
- **The band broke up.** The shipped game-over screen already reads "The band broke up." The first line Lucky says after loading a save that followed a game over: "We did not break up." Each later one cycles: "That was a hiatus." / "Creative differences. Resolved." / "Reunion tour." / "Farewell show. Fifth one." On the second game over only, the jingle registers (it is the dental plan in minor): Lucky: "Was that my dad's song?" Phoenix: "My brain thinks it was in minor." Lucky: "He'd love that."

### Chapter 3: The Merch Table Incident / Eat the Rich

- **The title card.** Lucky: "Pick one." Phoenix: "My brain thinks the parenthetical is a threat."
- **The contract.** After the third save in the chapter, inspecting the band agreement: Lucky: "Was this always this thick?" Phoenix: "My brain thinks it grows a page every time she sits down." Lucky: "Then stop sitting down." Bajonka sits down. One page. (The slice already counts `contractPages = 3 + saveCount`. This is the player's first hint the rider is in there.)
- **312 on rent.** EVICTION FINALIZED. Lucky types 312 into the rent portal. PAYMENT DECLINED: NOT A CURRENCY. Then Phoenix's canon line: "My brain thinks we are officially unhoused."
- **13/8, once.** Sticky's first win: the sting starts in 4/4, falls down the stairs in 13/8, lands on its feet. Lucky: "Better." (Shipped line.) The next win it is back to 4/4. Lucky: "It went back."

### Chapter 4: Battle of the Bands

- **The Judges' Table.** Phase 2 has no enemy HP. Lucky: "Where's your health bar?" The Tall Guitarist: "I don't have health. I have taste."
- One gag is enough here. The chapter already has the list.

### Chapter 5: The Acoustic Embargo

- **DAY 14.** Lucky has a tally scratched into the toaster. "Fourteen." The card is right for the first time all game. Lucky stares at it. "...It's right." Phoenix: "My brain thinks that's worse."
- **Under the text box.** Puddle Lucky moves through gaps, and the text box is a gap. Any time a dialogue box is open, Lucky can slide under it, and the laser grid does not scan the UI. It is the one place in the apartment the Vanguard does not watch. Lucky, from under it: "It's nice under here." Cyan Phoenix: "Get out from under the interface, Lucky."
- **The notary stamp.** The stamp for subsection C is "hidden in another menu." It is in Options, under TEXT SPEED: NOTARIZE, greyed out, help line "Available in a future update." That is why subsection C fails. Lucky: "That's a lie." Shannon: "Bureaucracy is a setting." Lucky: "Then turn it off." Shannon: "It's greyed out."
- **The slap.** The Osmium Dozen is won in silence, NOISE at zero. Then the win sting starts: D, E... Lucky's puddle reaches into the frame and slaps the speaker mid-stab: SQUELCH. The A never plays. It is the Chapter 1 pod-bay slap, aimed at the game's own music. Miss the window and NOISE spikes after the fight is already won. Lucky: "I've wanted to skip that since the van."

### Chapter 6: Planets Collide

- **GRAVITY.** On the rooftop, Kurt's bar reads GRAVITY. Lucky: "That's not a health bar." Phoenix: "My brain thinks it's a weather report." Lucky: "We'll hit it anyway."
- After ONE MORE CHORD Lucky says nothing. The game breaks its own wall from here (INTERMISSION, the cartridge card, the relabeled save) and Lucky is not in on it.

### Chapter 7: The Luthier of Debris

- **MEANWHILE.** The first card of Disc 2: MEANWHILE, IN THE DEBRIS FIELD. Lucky: "Meanwhile what? There's nothing else. We're it."
- **DISC 2.** First save. The slot reads DISC 2. Lucky: "Where's disc one?" Phoenix: "My brain thinks it was in the van."
- **The back of the cartridge.** The PLEASE TURN THE CARTRIDGE OVER card from the intermission is floating in the debris as a pickup. Lucky turns it over. The back is fine print, and it is the first page of the Terms & Conditions word bank: ALL RIGHTS RESERVED. NOT FOR RESALE. ANY RESEMBLANCE TO REAL PERSONS, LIVING OR DEAD, IS PURELY COINCIDENTAL. The last one is the longest clause in the bank and hits hardest. Lucky reads it twice. "Sure."
- **READ.** When READ appears on the save prompt, its help line is the Chapter 1 inspect line, struck through: ~~Lucky does not read it.~~

### Chapter 8: The Golem's Grief

- **The airship.** The first time the world map opens with Kurt as the vehicle. Lucky: "We have an airship." Diego: "He's not an airship, man. He's grieving." Lucky: "He's a grieving airship." Kurt: one note, very low.

### Chapter 9: Planet California

- **Onboarding, paid.** Dad's balloons are the beige tutorial boxes: same frame, same font. Lucky: "You wrote the tutorials." Dad: "Somebody had to onboard you." The first tutorial in the game taught Lucky to slap the tutorial.
- **Open enrollment.** When a balloon covers Lucky's HP: Lucky: "I can't see my health." Dad: "Your health is a benefit, Lucky. You can see it at open enrollment."
- **The roof.** As the tractor beam greys the commands one by one, Lucky reads them out like a roll call. "RIFF. STRUM. ITEM." Then RUN greys out. Lucky: "They took RUN." Phoenix: "My brain thinks we've never had RUN." (No scripted fight has ever let them flee.) Then the flyer, the billboard, the dog.

### Chapter 10: Because They Suck

- **The title card (optional).** Lucky: "Who named this one?" Nothing answers. Lucky still thinks it is about them. The chapter is the answer.
- **Brian hears the aside.** In Brian's fight, Lucky mutters at the menu: "Why is SPEAK even on here?" It counts as speaking. Brian: "Are you saying my home theater is outdated?!" Enraged. Even Lucky's asides are lines.
- **13/8 for good.** The Retention Unit's rounding error tears the Vanguard's grid open, and the win sting's grid goes with it. From this fight on it plays in 13/8, permanently. The first time: Lucky says nothing. They don't have to.

### Chapter 11: The Epstein Protocol

Lucky is clear. Lucky makes no gags. The win sting plays in 13/8 and nobody comments.

- **THE MORNING AFTER.** The card holds a beat longer than any card has. Nobody corrects it. Phoenix: "My brain thinks it was the afternoon." A pause. "My brain thinks Lucky usually does this part."
- **The band broke up.** After a game over, the first line on load is Phoenix's: "...We did not break up." They look at Lucky. Lucky says nothing.

### Chapter 12: The Elephant in the Room

- **Help lines.** From the load-in, every tutorial and how-to line is David's, in sound-guy shorthand: "Check." "On the four." "Less." Nothing is beige.
- **NOT COINCIDENTAL.** In Phase 2 Lucky counters with Terms & Conditions (canon), clear. If the player throws the cartridge clause at Trunk, it does 0, and the number reads NOT COINCIDENTAL. It is the only clause in the bank that fails on the last boss. The game has conceded the point a few turns before Lucky makes it.
- **No sting.** Trunk drops and the win sting does not play. Where it would go, Lucky counts in the next gig (canon: "One, two, three, four!"). The band is the fanfare.
- **312 on fuel.** In the gold cockpit, Lucky feeds 312 into the fuel slot. The gauge does not move. Then Phoenix reads the price, the canon last line, untouched.
- **THE END. (FOR NOW.)** The card goes up reading THE END. Lucky's pseudopod reaches into the frame and writes in (FOR NOW.). The card keeps it. Rule 2's one exception: the last card says what Lucky wants, and the game lets it stand.

**Phase 3 itself is canon and unchanged.** What this doc adds is the build: the gags stopped when Lucky went clear, so when Lucky's color comes back at the fourth wall, the gags come back with it, and this time Lucky is looking at the room.

## David plays from the booth *(canon, Lucky)*

The answer to the roadmap's question about the fifth slot in Chapter 12. David plays from the sound booth, and the booth is where the camera is: his row sits below the battle frame, toward the player. At the load-in Lucky asks "Can I get more me?" to the monitor wedge, the way singers do, so rule 1 holds. In Phase 3 "the band falls out, David last," so the last sound to drop comes from the player's side of the screen. When Lucky looks out for the first time, they are looking at the booth: at the person who turned them up, and at the player, at once.
